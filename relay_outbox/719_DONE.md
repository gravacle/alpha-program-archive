## Why I did not book FORCED

Exactly one candidate survives every testable require. That is the shape of FORCED and
DD3's wording would license it. **I declined**, because `tau_density` has not survived
and has not failed — it has not been evaluated. Booking FORCED would require treating
*"not yet a defined carrier"* as equivalent to *"eliminated"*, which converts inability
to see into a negative verdict. That is the one move the jurisdiction check forbids and
the discipline R9 exists to enforce. The asymmetry is displayed at full strength
instead; the call on it is yours.

**What would settle it, and it is cheap:** define `tau_density` as a complete
topological carrier and re-run the matrix. If it then violates R3 or R6, the verdict is
FORCED(`tau_norm`) by elimination. If it satisfies them, the choice is a genuine
two-way free one. Either outcome is a result, and this is cheaper than anything
remaining in the RA27 chain.
