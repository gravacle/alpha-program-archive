## THE LESSON — SHARPER THAN V005's

V005 said the generalisable finding was that a carriage proof scoped to the carried regions is blind
to the rewritten one. True, and insufficient.

**A SPAN DIGEST CERTIFIES FIDELITY, NOT COMPLETENESS.** Every digest in V005 verified. The bytes were
exactly the bytes named. The named bytes were not a whole block — and no digest can detect that. Only
a structural test can, so V006's generator adds the two that would have caught this with nobody
reading the file: fence balance, and exact-once occurrence of the complete block.

**The misbounded spans originated in the 1064 order and I executed them exactly.** That is where they
came from; it is not a defence. My generator was the thing positioned to catch it and did not ask the
question.

**Four completeness failures now, each in a different layer** — prose review; an aliased extractor; a
carriage proof blind where the editing happened; a span digest certifying the wrong span. The common
shape: each check was sound about what it measured and silent about its own scope.
