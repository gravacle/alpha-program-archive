## RULING 1 — "UNUSED" IS THE SEALED LINEAGE CLAUSE. THIS IS AN IDENTIFICATION, NOT A NEW DEFINITION.

Slot 18 requires "one **unused** structure-sensitive prediction"
(`STAGE6_PARENT_ACTION_AND_QSPEC_LEDGER_V002.md:119-139`). Q-23 established that "structure-sensitive" is
operationally defined and "unused" is not.

**RULED: `unused` IS the condition already sealed at
`BOUNDARY_INCIDENCE_DYNAMICS_PRINCIPLE_V011.md:2080-2089`** — "be absent as an outcome or target from the
complete historical lineage."

**AND "complete historical lineage" IS OPERATIONALIZED BY THE SCOPE V011 RULE 11 ALREADY DEFINES** for the
contamination audit: "search all accessible repository history, attachments, logs, prompts, tickets,
browser-history exports."

**WHY THIS FORM.** It adopts no new predicate. Both halves are sealed text that has been in the corpus
throughout; what was missing was the statement that they are the same condition. **An identification is a
weaker act than a definition, and the weaker act is the one available here** — a lane may not define its
own admissibility predicate, and neither should a principal invent one when the corpus already contains
the clause.

**THE TEST IT LICENSES:** a bounded search over the rule-11 scope, returning zero occurrences of the
candidate observable as an outcome or a target. Roots and exclusions stated, file lists reported.

**FALSIFIER F-U1:** if the rule-11 scope proves unbounded or unsearchable in practice, this identification
fails and `unused` returns to undefined. Report that rather than substituting a narrower scope.

**FALSIFIER F-U2:** the stronger reading — that the observable was never *consumed by the derivation*,
whether or not it appears in the record — was considered and NOT adopted. If a candidate passes the
lineage test but is later shown to have shaped the construction, this ruling did not protect against it,
and that case must be escalated rather than absorbed.

## RULING 2 — A THIRD ADMISSIBILITY CLAUSE IS ADDED

Established by `FINDING_D_TEST_DEGENERATE_ON_PRECISION_OBSERVABLES_2026-07-30.md`: on the electron
magnetic anomaly — the only family with an available comparator payload — `D >= 5` is passed by a refuted
theory and failed by a correct one, because `sigma_comp` and `sigma_meas` are minute and any difference
large enough to pass is already excluded by measurement.

**RULED — CLAUSE 3, ADDED TO `structure-sensitive` AND `unused`:**

> An observable is admissible only if **the comparator's own predictive uncertainty is not so far below
> any plausible BID deviation that a `D >= 5` difference would be automatically in conflict with
> established measurement.**

**IT IS A CONDITION ON COMPARATOR PRECISION, NEVER ON A MEASURED CENTRAL VALUE.** That is deliberate and
it is what keeps it inside the fences: `D` already admits `sigma_meas` into its denominator, so admitting
comparator and measurement *uncertainties* into an admissibility test is the same posture the protocol
already takes. **No measured central value enters, and no comparison to one is performed.**

**APPLIED AT THE ELIGIBILITY STAGE, BEFORE THE BEACON SELECTS.** Not at collection, so the universe need
not be re-collected; and not after selection, which would be selection-by-outcome.

**THE COST, STATED RATHER THAN DISCOVERED:** this will shrink an already thin eligible set, **possibly to
zero.** `V011` says an empty eligible set fails closed. **That is an honest outcome and not a defect** —
a terminal claim resting on an observable where the test cannot discriminate would be worth less than no
claim.

**FALSIFIER F-C3:** if clause 3 cannot be applied without consulting a measured central value, it
breaches the comparison fence and must be withdrawn. Report the breach; do not soften the clause to fit.
