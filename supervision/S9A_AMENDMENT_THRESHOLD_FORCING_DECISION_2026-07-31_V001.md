## 2. WHY — INHERITED ERROR, NOT A DELIBERATE HIGHER BAR

The wording was written while the standing principle held that **"only overdetermination forces a
number."** *** Q-65 REFUTED THAT PRINCIPLE: a require-shaped condition with derived existence and
uniqueness forces a number on its own. The slogan fused forcing (a fact about the admissible set) with
anti-fitting (a fact about how we know the point was not chosen). *** S9-A inherited the error rather than
expressing a choice; nothing in its registration gives a rationale for the stronger demand.

**AND THE UNAMENDED WORDING WAS ACTIVELY HARMFUL.** Q-61 found the corpus has **zero live pairs.** *** SO
S9-A AS WRITTEN WAS PRESENTLY UNSATISFIABLE, AND ANY LANE TOLD TO DISCHARGE IT WOULD HUNT FOR A PAIR — THE
EXACT DETOUR THE PROGRAM ALREADY TOOK ONCE, WRITTEN INTO A SEALED OBLIGATION WHERE IT WOULD REPEAT ITSELF
WITHOUT ANYONE CHOOSING IT. ***

**THE PRINCIPAL'S GROUNDS, RECORDED IN HIS OWN TERMS:** the whole program is rooted in **thresholds**. Q-71
established the forcing shape is the threshold, and §5.3 of the `Gamma_K` construction spec is already a
complete uniqueness gate built for exactly that. **In a threshold-rooted architecture overdetermination is
not a higher grade waiting to be reached; it is a category that does not naturally arise.**

## 3. WHAT REPLACES IT — AND WHAT IS NOT RETAINED

**S9-A is discharged by the §5.3 route:** one simple positive root, no inequivalent admitted positive root,
no continuous modulus, **uniqueness not obtained by narrowing the admitted family after seeing the root**,
and the root **solved once and independently reproduced.**

*** OVERDETERMINATION IS NOT RETAINED AS AN AVAILABLE STRONGER GRADE TO PURSUE. *** Holding it open as an
aspiration is what sent lanes at an empty route. **It is not a target, not a bonus, and not a fallback.**

## 4. THE ONE THING RECORDED INSTEAD — A DISCLOSURE, NOT A GOAL

*** THE SINGLE-CONDITION ROUTE CARRIES A RESIDUAL RISK IT CANNOT SELF-CHECK, AND THE PROGRAM MUST SAY SO
RATHER THAN HOLD A DOOR OPEN. ***

Q-65 established precisely what redundancy alone supplies: **independent corroboration against a WRONG
condition — error-risk, not fit-risk.** The threshold route's own checks do not cover this. **§5.3's
"solved once and independently reproduced" catches computational and implementation error. It cannot catch
the condition itself being physically wrong, because there is nothing to disagree with it.**

```text
S9A_RESIDUAL_ERROR_RISK_DISCLOSED = true
S9A_RESIDUAL_ERROR_RISK_CLOSED    = false | TYPE-C
    constraint: no second independent condition exists in the corpus (Q-61, zero live pairs)
    release:    an independently-motivated second condition landing on the same quantity
```

**ANY REPORTED ALPHA MUST CARRY THIS DISCLOSURE.** A derivation forced by one derived unique condition is
a genuine derivation, **and it is singly-attested (Q-65). If that condition is wrong rather than merely
unproven, nothing in this route detects it.**

---

## 5. WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

**It does not lower the anti-fit standard.** Q-65's certificates, freeze-before-root-finding, the
target-entry sweep, and §5.3's prohibition on narrowing the family after seeing the root **all stand
unchanged.** *** THE STANDARD IS NOT BEING RELAXED SO THE PROGRAM CAN PASS IT; A DEMAND THAT WAS NEVER
REQUIRED IS BEING WITHDRAWN, AND THE COST OF WITHDRAWING IT IS DISCLOSED. ***

It authorizes no claim, discharges no obligation, and moves no flag. **S9-A remains open.**
