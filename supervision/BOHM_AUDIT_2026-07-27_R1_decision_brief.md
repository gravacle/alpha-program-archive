## 1. WHAT R1 IS

The number the program is trying to reach is sourced from a **curvature** — a second derivative, at the origin, of a quantity that is only defined *after* you take an infinite-volume limit (`BOUNDARY_INCIDENCE_DYNAMICS_PRINCIPLE_V011.md:1292-1300`: "evaluated **after** the thermodynamic/linked-cluster limit"). To get that object today, the program must prove a strong theorem, T7(iii), which says the whole expansion converges on a disc of some derived radius, that the *differentiated* series also converges, and that differentiating-then-taking-the-limit gives the same answer as taking-the-limit-then-differentiating (`STAGE8_T7_CONNECTED_LINKED_CLUSTER_MAJORANT_DERIVATION_SPEC_V001.md:247-273`). R1 proposes to skip all of that by **defining** the curvature to be the limit of the finite-volume curvatures, and requiring only that this one object exist and not depend on how space is chopped up (`STAGE8_RL2B_OVER_CHARTER_AND_BLIND_ROUTE_FINDING_V001.md:213-215`). The two orders of operation are the two sides of one sealed equation — `lim_K [ d_+ d_- (-log Ghat_K) / N_4(K) ]|_(0,0) = d_+ d_- (-gamma_inf)|_(0,0)` (`STAGE8_T7_COMPLETED_CONDITIONED_DUHAMEL_IDENTITY_AND_INTERCHANGE_SCHEMA_SPEC_V001.md:560-562`) — and R1 adopts the left side as the definition and deletes the obligation to prove it equals the right.

## 2. THE DECISION

**The principal would be deciding whether to** replace a sealed requirement that the record theory *possess* a connected linked-cluster thermodynamic density and that the Hessian of the limit *equal* the limit of the Hessians, with a stipulation that the limit of the Hessians **is** the intensive Hessian — thereby converting T7(iv) from a cross-check into a definition, and spending, before its trigger, a fallback that has already been ruled `NOT_CHARTERED` in sealed text.
