adopted postulate" — on which reading F2 has not fired, because S9-A has never been attempted. That is
defensible in principle, since slot 9's obligation genuinely changed under Q-19 and a falsifier whose

because a retirement happened to move its referent. **A declared label may not survive on a

obligation. That is what "frozen" means.
