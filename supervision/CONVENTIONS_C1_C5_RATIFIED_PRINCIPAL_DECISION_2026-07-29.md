# CONSTRUCTION CONVENTIONS C1-C5 — RATIFIED (PRINCIPAL DECISION, 2026-07-29)

PROVENANCE: A PRINCIPAL ACT, ruled 2026-07-29, session e268883f. All five conventions
RATIFIED AS WRITTEN in `STAGE8_CONSTRUCTION_CONVENTIONS_C1_C5_PROPOSED_RULES_V001.md`
(cleanroom; sidecar verified). They were Einstein's working practice, recorded in its
handoff §A.2 as rules that existed nowhere; Codex drafted them as PROPOSED; they are now
adopted rules of the program.

RATIFIED:
C1. A lane finding does not receive a Part 2 (ruled) row merely because it is a finding.
    Part 2 is reserved for ruled items. Lane findings may be sealed, cited, mirrored and
    used as authorities in scope, but are not indexed as principal rulings absent a ruling.
    (Prevents the R-6/R-7/R-8 defect: lane determinations filed under "RULED".)
C2. A named obstruction at a file:line is a SEALABLE RESULT CLASS. A failed attempt is a
    result when its obstruction is located. (This is what makes T7(ii), C-L1 and H-ANGLE
    count as results rather than as nothing.)
C3. When a sealed clause asserts a register row exists and it does not, the construction
    lane WRITES THE MISSING ROW rather than editing the sealed clause — provided the row
    faithfully indexes an existing authority and manufactures no ruling. If no authority
    supports the row, the lane STOPS AND REPORTS rather than inventing one.
C4. A declined referral is provenance and goes on the face of the result.
C5. Carrier-indexed numbers may be used REFUTATIONALLY (to decide a negative existential)
    while remaining F'-5-inadmissible as constants in any bound. The use must be declared.

NOTE ON C3, disclosed: Einstein flagged C3 as "defensible either way." It is ratified with
its own guard intact — the stop-and-report clause is the operative limit, and C3 may not be
used to paper over a sealed clause that is simply wrong. If a lane finds itself writing a
row to rescue a clause that no authority supports, that is the STOP case, not the C3 case.
