## 1. THE TARGET

**What relationship, if any, holds between gravitational structure, electromagnetic structure,
and alpha, in the process by which quantum cells form and records come into being?**

Stated so it can fail. "Derive alpha" cannot fail — it can only be unfinished, which is the
condition its predecessor stayed in for months. This target admits a clean negative: *no such
relationship is exhibited by any process we can build.*

## 2. THE FOUNDING REFRAME — THE PRINCIPAL'S, 2026-08-16

**ALPHA IS AN INPUT, NOT A TARGET.** It is a feature the process must accommodate, alongside
electromagnetism. We do not compute it. We require any candidate process to have a place
where a dimensionless coupling of its kind lives.

Three consequences, and the first is the reason to do this at all.

**(a) The predecessor's central obstruction becomes a prediction.** Its partition theorem
established that the magnitude is **allow-side** — permitted by the closure's clauses, forced
by nothing. That was read as the wall. Under this reframe it is the **expected structure**: a
process that leaves the coupling's slot open is exactly what a process should look like when
the coupling is supplied from outside. The strongest negative result the predecessor produced
is, on this footing, its strongest positive one.

**(b) The anti-fitting apparatus dissolves.** Three fences, a frozen release condition with
three ordered gates, a value-blind lane and a one-shot comparison protocol all exist because
computing alpha risks fitting to a known answer. **If alpha is an input there is nothing to
fit.** That machinery is not relaxed; its rationale is gone.

**(c) It matches practice.** No one derived the fine-structure constant. Using measured
constants as constraints on structure is ordinary; deriving them is the exotic ambition, and
that ambition returned FREE.

## 3. THE ONE DISCIPLINE THAT SURVIVES — STRUCTURE, NOT INSERTION

Alpha may constrain **structure**. It may not be **inserted as a parameter**.

- **Legitimate:** "the process must carry a free dimensionless coupling of this type, entering
  at this site, with these consequences."
- **Barred:** setting a coupling to a measured value and reading off what follows.

This is the old fitting risk in a new costume: a process designed around alpha would explain
alpha and mean nothing. This distinction replaces the entire fence apparatus and is the only
part of it carried forward.

## 4. SIX DESIGN PRINCIPLES, EACH EARNED FROM A NAMED FAILURE

**P1 — BUILD FIRST, CHARACTERIZE SECOND.** The predecessor characterized for months and
reached three-of-three uniqueness against **zero-of-three existence** — exactly what the
external survey's ranked law predicts for sharpening characterizations, since *existence is
bought by a compactness-type property of the ambient, never by the sharpness of the
characterization*. The unit of work here is a **construction attempt**, not a question.

**P2 — THE METRIC IS CONSTRUCTIONS COMPLETED.** Closed routes do not increment progress.
Eleven were closed in one day and the position did not move.

**P3 — EXTERNAL CONTACT AT EVERY STAGE.** The predecessor ran exactly one external check, at
the very end, and it returned two of fourteen with both on authored content. Every stage here
names something known independently that the construction must reproduce. Not alpha —
something cheap.

**P4 — DECLARE THE DECOMPOSITION.** No mechanism in six surveyed traditions derives one and
every one requires one. So it is stated, early and in the open: *this is the split we
stipulate, this is why, this is what changes if it differs.* Auditable rather than smuggled in
behind an imperative.

**P5 — FINITENESS IS THE EXISTENCE ENGINE.** It is the one structural advantage this setting
holds over the entire continuum literature. Every construction is attempted finite first.
Continuum questions are deferred or dropped, never inherited.

**P6 — ANCHORS MANDATORY, MECHANICALLY.** No term enters a governing clause without a pointer.
The predecessor's dominant documentation failure was that things travelled by quotation and
lost their anchors — thirteen instances in a single day, one of them a governing term quoted
twenty-nine times with no definition anywhere.

## 5. THE STAGE SEQUENCE

### STAGE 1 — BUILD ONE CARRIER, CONCRETELY
A **specific** finite oriented regular CW complex of dimension at most two: named cells, an
incidence structure, an orientation, one root. Over it, the object the predecessor already
adopted by ruling — a one-dimensional Hermitian fiber at each vertex and a discrete unitary
connection on edges. Small enough to compute on by hand and by machine.
**Deliverable:** the complex and its connection, written down explicitly, with holonomies
around every face computed.
**Falsifier:** none — this is construction, not claim. If it cannot be written down, the
program stops at once and that is a result.

### STAGE 2 — WRITE THE FORMATION CONDITION ON THAT CARRIER'S OWN DATA
Attempt to state the onset condition using only the complex, its fibers and its connection.
The predecessor's gate fires on an orthogonality condition; its Hilbert space is written as
source, designated record subsystem and required environment — **a split the complex itself
does not contain**, introduced by an imperative.
**Deliverable:** either the condition written on the carrier's own data, or an explicit
statement of exactly what must be added and why.
**Falsifier, and it is the program's first real one:** if the condition cannot be stated
without a stipulated split, say so and declare the split under P4. Do not disguise it.

### STAGE 3 — RUN IT
Unitary dynamics, the condition, and observe. Does anything durable appear? Under what
conditions? **This is the step the predecessor never took in fourteen thousand files.**
**Falsifier:** nothing forms, under any admissible condition, on any carrier in the family.
That kills the approach and it kills it in weeks.

### STAGE 4 — VARY THE ELECTROMAGNETIC STRUCTURE AND WATCH
Change the connection: holonomies, winding number, charge assignment. **Does formation
change, and how?** A gravity/EM/formation relationship, if it exists, shows itself here — as a
**dependence**, not as a derivation.
**Falsifier:** formation is wholly insensitive to the connection. Then EM has no role in
formation on this construction, which is a genuine and publishable negative.

### STAGE 5 — LOOK FOR THE SLOT
Only now: does the running construction carry a **free dimensionless coupling** — a quantity
the structure permits and does not fix? Where does it enter, and what does it govern?
**Falsifier:** the construction fixes every dimensionless quantity it contains. Then it cannot
accommodate alpha as an input and it is the wrong process.

### STAGE 6 — ONLY THEN, FORCING
Ask what is forced. Never before something is running.
