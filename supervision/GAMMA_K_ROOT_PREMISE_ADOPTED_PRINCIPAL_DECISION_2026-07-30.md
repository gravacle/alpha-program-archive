## THE REASONING, AND IT REFRAMES WHAT AN ADOPTION IS

The question was posed as adopt-versus-refuse, with testing as a mitigation. **That framing was wrong and
the principal corrected it.** In science one adopts a hypothesis IN ORDER TO test it. The adoption is not
a cost paid to proceed — **it is the act that makes the thing falsifiable.** A premise never stated is a
premise never refutable.

So the operative question is not whether a premise is proven. It is: **is this a hypothesis with a live
test attached, or an assumption nobody will ever check?**
