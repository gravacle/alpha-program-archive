## 5. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR "DO THE INGREDIENTS EXIST"

**The ingredients for a forced result are not present in the corpus as it stands.**

Four independent classes have now been checked and all four return negative:
1. **Symmetries** — enumerated and ruled out by the corpus's own ledger (ABSOLUTE_STIFFNESS_SELECTOR_ROUTE_LEDGER_V003.md:23-27).
2. **The signature mechanism** — the sealed history weights were computed exactly and `do not, by themselves, provide a canonical finite continuum electromagnetic normalization` (COMPLETE_QSPEC_ABSOLUTE_SCALE_AND_CONTINUUM_PREREQUISITE_AUDIT_V001.md:117).
3. **A blind degrees-of-freedom count** — zero constraints on the deciding axis.
4. **Consistency conditions** — this sweep. Shape-only or assumed throughout.

Two things make this negative stronger than "we haven't looked hard enough":

**Executed counterexamples, not absences of proof.** Three of them, independently constructed: two covariant local nets with different responses (R3_4_PARENT_STATE_COVARIANCE_ADJUDICATION_RESULT_V001.md:64); an infinite family of measures satisfying a five-condition stack (LOCAL_COVARIANT_CELL_MEASURE_SELECTOR_RESULT_V001.md:53-58); and the Pauli pair — two parameter-free, `K_bare=0`, Lorentz- and CPT-covariant parent actions with different finite responses (COMPLETE_PARENT_ACTION_UNDERDETERMINATION_GATE_V001.md:73). Plus a fourth, on the exact angle of the entropic hope: MICROSCOPIC_EXHAUSTION_IDENTIFIABILITY_GATE_V001.md:79-81.

**A type argument, flagged as analysis and not corpus text.** The deformation that must be excluded — a finite `c_R F^2` term — is itself local, causal, Lorentz-covariant, gauge-invariant, finite, regulator-independent and packing-independent. So no condition in the locality / covariance / support / positivity / independence class can be *violated* by adding it. **The condition class is closed under the deformation it would have to exclude.** That is why every item here is preserved or improved by the addition, and it is a structural reason rather than a contingent gap.

**What this is not.** This is a statement about *this corpus*, not about nature and not a no-go theorem. Nothing above shows that no consistency condition could ever constrain this coefficient. Two of the four candidate routes are of the right kind and simply undelivered; the corpus itself keeps them open with honest status flags. "Not present" and "cannot exist" are different claims and only the first is supported.

One diagnosis worth stating plainly, because it recurs everywhere: **this corpus has consistency-as-*check* throughout and consistency-as-*constraint* nowhere.** `overdetermin` returns 0 files. The mutation-invariance requirement at FINITE_RECORD_CELL_FLUX_RESPONSE_PROTOCOL_V001.md:97-99 looks bootstrap-shaped, but both computations run from the same sealed Q_cell — it can falsify a candidate, not select a value. The corpus's own general statement of the point: `Well-posedness of a quantifier is precisely not discharge of what is quantified.` (STAGE8_OD3_LEVER_DETERMINATION_V001.md:108).

## 6. WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE ADDED — typed, not planned

Five type-level statements. None is a rescue route; each says what *kind* of object is missing.

1. **An equality, not an inequality.** Everything derived here is one-sided: `C_R <= 1`, `J_FS,rel >= pi hbar/2`, `|Z| <= 1`, `K_R > 0`, `g >= 0`. In each case the magnitude arrives only by adopting saturation. A coefficient is pinned by a two-sided sandwich or by a genuine equality, and the corpus's one exact equality (the one-cell kernel) is saturated by construction and carries no stiffness.
2. **A quantity whose *finiteness* depends monotonically on the coefficient**, so the finiteness threshold falls at an interior value rather than an endpoint. The corpus has one working instance of this mechanism — Hilbert–Schmidt finiteness fixing a Schatten-2 exponent in R-L2b — and it works because refinement supplies a one-parameter family along which finiteness can be demanded uniformly. *(That exponent is written "alpha" in the R-L2b material and is **not** the fine-structure constant.)* The Thomson stiffness is a scale-free pure number with no refinement behaviour, and `K_bare = 0` deletes a term rather than creating such a family.
3. **A second, oppositely-directed bound** to pair with contractivity. None exists; the machinery that would supply one returns zero hits under every phrasing.
4. **An occupant for slot 8** — `the rule that forbids an independent finite \`c_R F^2\` mutation.` (FINITE_RECORD_CELL_FLUX_RESPONSE_PROTOCOL_V001.md:24). The slot is reserved in at least five artifacts and empty in all five. It requires a condition that is **violated by adding a small positive `c_R`**. Every condition in this corpus is preserved or improved by that addition. That is the sharpest available statement of what is missing.
5. **Most likely: not a consistency condition at all, but a construction.** The corpus's own three sufficient outcomes for closing the underdetermination are all constructions — a first-order minimality theorem, a total-space geometric action, or a unique gauge-covariant finite update (COMPLETE_PARENT_ACTION_UNDERDETERMINATION_GATE_V001.md:80-88), with :91 `Merely declaring one option "minimal" is not a derivation.` The typed-form gate's own preferred route for the missing ratio is "a complete microscopic dilation … because it can calculate the ratio without declaring an equality." The corpus's considered judgment, repeatedly and independently reached, is that **calculation rather than consistency is where the number must come from.**
