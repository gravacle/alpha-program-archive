## THE REASONING, RECORDED SO THE RULING CAN BE ATTACKED

1. **`K_bare = 0` is the whole point.** With zero bare `F^2` there is no counterterm available to
   absorb anything, so the induced coefficient IS whatever the regulated calculation produces. A
   demand for total regulator-independence would demand that the coupling not depend on the object
   that generates it. That is not a strong condition; it is an inconsistent one.
2. **Scheme-independence and condition-freeness are different claims.** Regulator independence
   properly means the physics does not depend on the cutoff SCHEME. It has never meant that no
   finite condition is imposed — in any renormalized theory a dimensionless coupling is defined by
   a condition. Slot 12 as written conflated the two.
3. **This is an instance of the binding methodological rule** (principal, 2026-07-29): "DO NOT LET
   THE MACHINERY DICTATE THE PHYSICS. 'Our expansion breaks down here' is NOT the claim 'the
   physics is singular here.'" Slot 12's absolute form is a machinery-shaped requirement standing
   in front of a physical mechanism.
4. **It preserves the allow/require boundary rather than relaxing it.** The permitted condition
   must be FORCED. Nothing here licenses picking a scheme, a scale, or a subtraction point.
