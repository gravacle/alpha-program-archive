# OBSERVATIONS REGISTER — V001

**PURPOSE, AND WHY IT IS SEPARATE FROM THE QUESTIONS REGISTER.** The questions register records what is
SETTLED. This one records what was SEEN and whose significance is UNCLAIMED.

**THE FAILURE IT EXISTS TO PREVENT.** In this program the standard move on an observation is: ask which
existing framework it fits, find it does not, record a closure. That is correct when the framework's
verdict is a REFUTATION. **It is wrong when the verdict is a FAILURE TO FIT** — and in genuinely new
territory, a fit failure is weak evidence about the observation and strong evidence about the framework.

**THE DISTINCTION, AND IT IS THE WHOLE POINT:**
- **REFUTATION** — a theorem forecloses it. Q-01: index-weighting versus dimension-weighting forces
  `1/23040 = 0`, and no reframing survives. Q-13's slot classification. **These stay closed.**
- **FAILURE TO FIT** — "does not satisfy an existing theorem's hypotheses", or "is not the object another
  spec requires." **These belong here, with their content extracted rather than discarded.**

**WHY NOW (2026-07-30).** Three of the program's commitments are genuinely new: the record as a primitive
algebraic sector; couplings as impedances of ACCESS CONDITIONS rather than Lagrangian normalizations; and
fixing a coupling by an ONSET condition — the value at which a durable record first forms. **No prior
framework envisioned any of these**, so frameworks that did not envision them will systematically reject
their content. Standard machinery — quasifree states, GNS, crossed products, modular theory, heat kernels
— is decades old and carries no risk. The risk is in reading a fit failure as a verdict.

**ENTRY FORM.** What was observed · where it came from · what framework it failed and why that was filed as
a closure · **WHAT IT WOULD MEAN AS THEORY CONTENT** · what would have to be true for it to be right ·
status. **No entry here adopts, derives, or authorizes anything.**

   belongs in the questions register. Failing to satisfy a framework's hypotheses, or not being the object
   some spec requires, is a FIT FAILURE and belongs here.
2. **Extract the content before filing the closure.** The entry must say what the observation would mean if
   taken as theory content, not only which box it failed.
3. **Never let an entry here license anything.** Unclaimed means unclaimed: no adoption, no derivation, no
   authorization.
4. **Revisit when the framework changes.** An observation that failed to fit an object may fit its
   successor — that is the whole reason for keeping it.
