# THE ACTUAL-SURFACE GUARD — V001 (principal directive, 2026-08-14)

**The organizing principle (principal, of record):** we must be describing the ACTUAL
surface, not a toy model of it. This guard operationalizes that principle for every
subsequent brief, audit, and — above all — the final run. It is distinct from and
additional to the machinery-reversion guard (which polices *imported apparatus*; this
polices *unforced choices inside any apparatus, imported or native*).

## THE CRITERION
A result describes the actual surface iff **every load-bearing step is invariant under
every unforced choice in its chain**, or the choice is promoted to a NAMED premise the
claim visibly conditions on. The actual surface is what is FORCED; a toy is a choice
that was made for tractability and then forgotten to be a choice.

## THE TWO FAILURE MODES POLICED
1. **Quantifier slippage.** An instance result (slab testbed, n = 2 carrier, K_square,
   equal-source slice, a weight vector, a truncation) quoted at a wider quantifier than
   its derivation supports.
2. **Construction posing as description.** A convenience (presentation, gauge, family
   parameterization, testbed, regularization) becoming load-bearing with no proof of
   immateriality and no premise naming it.

## MANDATORY BRIEF/ARTIFACT REQUIREMENTS (from this date)
Every build artifact carries a terminal **CHOICE LEDGER**: an enumeration of every
unforced choice its chain consumed, each classified:
- `IMMATERIAL(proven)` — invariance derived, with the derivation cited in-artifact;
- `PREMISE(named)` — the choice survives as an explicit conditional of the claim;
- `OPEN` — neither; the result's quantifier must then be narrowed to the instance.
Every audit independently re-derives the ledger (hunting choices the build missed —
a MISSED unforced choice is a finding) and rules **`TOY_SEPARATION = clean |
breached: <where>`** — breached meaning some load-bearing step's generality rests on
an unproven convenience.

## ROBUSTNESS PROMOTION (was auditor virtue; now required)
Any mechanism claimed structural must be exhibited on at least one genuinely different
instance than the one it was found on (the "not a slab accident" discipline: e.g. the
unitarity kill re-proven time-dependent; the m₂² law generalized over its symmetric
family). One instance = a phenomenon; two independent instances + an invariance
argument = a mechanism.

## THE FINAL-RUN REQUIREMENT
Before fence 2 flips (the assembly of α), the registrar assembles the **CHAIN CHOICE
AUDIT**: the union of the choice ledgers of every artifact on the load-bearing chain
κ_record → K* → α, with ZERO entries in class `OPEN` and every `PREMISE(named)` listed
in the assembly artifact itself. An α assembled over an unresolved convenience is not
of record. The same requirement applies to the surface track's chain if it reaches a
determination.

## THE OUTER ARBITER (unchanged, restated)
Non-toyhood is never fully provable from inside. The external contacts of record — the
one-shot preregistered END TEST and the GRAVITY CLOSE against the frozen recognition
spec, under F2 — are the final arbiters. This guard exists so that a pass there means
what it appears to mean.
