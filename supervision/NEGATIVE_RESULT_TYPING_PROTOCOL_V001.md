## 1. THE PROBLEM, STATED PLAINLY

**The principal:** *"We need to be able to test and not inject artificial failure cases that are not
necessarily applicable."*

This corpus reports **four different things** in one vocabulary — `= false`, `NOT FOUND`, `NOT DERIVED`,
`NOT YET AUDITED`. They look identical in a flag block. **They are not remotely the same claim, and three
of the four say nothing about physics.**

*** THE HARM IS SPECIFIC: A NEGATIVE PRODUCED BY A CONSTRAINT GETS READ AS A RESULT ABOUT THE WORLD. ***
The program then spends lanes chasing it.

**IT HAPPENED THREE TIMES TODAY.**
- Lane 1 reported the induced response operator "does not exist." **Ninety minutes later the Q-48 fence
  audit found a finite operator response bundle DERIVED and confirmed by two hostile reviews** (Q-50). The
  original negative was true only within bounded roots under a naming convention. **A theory was being
  built on it.**
- The proper-time floor's status was carried secondhand because a fence barred the primary text. **The
  reviewer read the resulting mismatch as staleness and filed a defect that did not exist** (Q-45 → Q-46
  → Q-49).
- `C_R = 1` carried "no physical falsifier is frozen." **That reads as untestable. Lane 2 then showed a
  falsifier IS designable** (paste 154). "None frozen" was a bookkeeping fact about the corpus, and it was
  one inference away from being reported as a premise that cannot be tested.

## 2. THE FOUR TYPES

**TYPE-R — REFUTED.** A test capable of failing was executed and returned evidence against the claim.
*** THIS IS THE ONLY TYPE THAT IS PHYSICAL CONTENT. ***
Example: OBS-05 — `lambda != 1` fails the retraction axiom (Q-44).

**TYPE-U — UNBUILT.** The object has not been constructed. **Says nothing about whether it can be.** Must
name what would build it.
Example: the raw-correlator-to-retarded-Hessian map (Q-51).

**TYPE-S — SCOPE-EMPTY.** A bounded search returned nothing. **Must record roots, exclusions, fences in
force, and the exact query.** Absence from a scope is not absence from the program, and neither is absence
from physics.
Example: "no artifact stating this premise exists" in the bounded prior inventory.

**TYPE-C — CONSTRAINT-BLOCKED.** A constraint prevented the check from running. Must name the constraint
and its release condition.
Example: the CAR-named source excluded from lane reading.
*** UNDER Q-52 THIS TYPE IS A GOVERNANCE DEFECT, NOT A RESULT. LOG IT AND FIX THE CONSTRAINT. ***

## 3. THE RULES

1. **Every `= false` flag and every "not found" carries its type.** Untyped negatives are not findings.

2. *** ONLY TYPE-R MAY BE CITED AS PHYSICAL CONTENT, USED TO KILL A THEORY OR OBSERVATION, OR COUNTED AS
   A REFUTATION. *** TYPE-U, TYPE-S and TYPE-C are work-state, not world-state.

3. **A TYPE-S negative cited without its scope is VOID.** Scope means: roots searched, exclusions applied,
   fences in force, query used. **This is already done well in places and must be universal** — it is
   exactly what made the floor-entry error detectable.

4. **A test whose failure condition depends on untested premises yields a CONDITIONAL negative**, typed
   with the premises it rests on. **With 17 of 26 premises untested (Q-45), most negatives on the value
   path are conditional and must say so.**

5. *** A NEGATIVE CARRIES THE SAME EVIDENTIARY BURDEN AS A POSITIVE. *** Reporting "not found" requires
   stating the search. **Today the cheapest legitimate action is to report an absence, which biases the
   whole corpus toward negatives** — Einstein found theory candidate 001 "under-argued in its own favour
   on seven stages" for exactly this reason.

6. **"NOT YET AUDITED" and "NOT YET OPENED" are TODO items, not statuses.** They may not appear in a flag
   block as though they were findings.

## 4. THE APPLICABILITY TEST — THE PRINCIPAL'S ACTUAL POINT

Before any failure case is accepted, it must answer: *** DOES THIS FAILURE CONDITION EVEN APPLY? ***

A test may not manufacture a failure out of a requirement that is itself conditional or unmotivated. Two
live instances:

- **The `C_R = 1` falsifier requires a completed source-record-gravity action.** Q-51 says the layer
  upstream of it cannot be started. **So the test cannot run — but that is a fact about the test's
  prerequisites, NOT evidence about marginal self-gravitation.** It must never be recorded as though
  `C_R = 1` had survived or failed anything.
- **Theory candidate 001's closures were graded against "does sealed text specify X."** Einstein correctly
  reclassified this as **"has-not-yet, not cannot."** An absence of specification is TYPE-S or TYPE-U, and
  cannot support a "there is no place left" claim.

**RULE:** a failure condition resting on a premise that is untested, inapplicable, or itself unbuilt
produces **NO_VERDICT**, not a failure. **NO_VERDICT is an honest and reportable outcome.**
