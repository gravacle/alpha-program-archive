## 1. THE RULING, IN THE PRINCIPAL'S WORDS

> "We don't want constraints that are preventing the testing of new theories or new physical
> properties. We need to be able to test those and the machinery needs to permit that."

*** NO CONSTRAINT IN THIS PROGRAM MAY PREVENT A NEW THEORY OR A NEW PHYSICAL PROPERTY FROM BEING
TESTED. *** Where a governance constraint and a test conflict, **the constraint yields.**

---

## 2. THE LINE THIS DRAWS

The program's constraints divide into two kinds, and they have been treated as one.

**LEGITIMATE — CONSTRAINTS AGAINST FABRICATION. THESE ALL STAND.**
- No computing alpha, kappa_record, kappa_Thomson, a coupling, a radius, a scale, a root, an
  eigenvalue or a beta function **as a claim**.
- No comparison to any measured constant.
- Report refutations; never repair them.
- Never touch `a32_holdout/custodian_private/`.
- Seal before execution; declare premises at the outset (F-GK3).
- Every adoption carries its test or is marked untested (Q-37).

**ILLEGITIMATE — CONSTRAINTS AGAINST TESTING. THESE FALL.**
- **A charter that names one "sole construction target" and thereby forbids specifying anything else.**
- Any fence with no written release condition (already corrected once, Q-48).
- Any rule under which a missing object cannot be specified because specifying it is out of scope.
- Any reading of F-GK3 under which declaring a premise is treated as forbidden rather than as
  required disclosure.

**THE DISTINGUISHING QUESTION:** does the constraint stop us from claiming something we have not
earned, or does it stop us from finding out whether something is true? **The first stands. The second
falls.**

---

## 3. WHAT CHANGES IMMEDIATELY

**(a) THE `Gamma_K` CHARTER IS AMENDED. `Gamma_K` IS NO LONGER THE SOLE CONSTRUCTION TARGET.** Q-51
established that four objects sit upstream of it — the complete BR/CTP fluctuation-response operator,
the exact induced kernel, the raw-correlator-to-retarded-Hessian map `G -> H_R[G]`, and the covariant
local projector — and that `Gamma_K` cannot be started until they exist. **The response-extraction
layer is hereby an authorized construction target in its own right.**

**(b) A LANE MAY SPECIFY A MISSING OBJECT IN ORDER TO TEST IT.** Previously the reviewer instructed
lanes not to supply what is absent. **That instruction is withdrawn where the purpose is a test.** A
lane may write a specification, provided it is declared at the outset, marked `derived = false`, and
**never reported as derived.**

**(c) THEORY CANDIDATES AND OBSERVATIONS ARE TESTABLE OBJECTS AND MAY BE GIVEN LANE TIME.** They are
not required to be fitted into an existing framework first. The observations register exists because
a framework that never envisioned this work cannot be the gatekeeper of what may be examined.

**(d) A PREMISE MAY BE VARIED TO TEST IT.** Testing `C_R = 1` requires considering `C_R != 1`; testing
`tau = 1` requires the competitor family. **Considering an alternative is not adopting it**, and no
fence is violated by holding a premise's negation long enough to see what fails.

## 4. THE FAILURE MODE THIS CREATES, AND ITS GUARD

*** "PERMITS TESTING" MUST NOT DECAY INTO "PERMITS ASSUMING." *** The whole value of this program's
discipline is that nothing is reported as earned when it was supplied. The guard is unchanged and now
carries more weight:

- **Q-37:** every adoption carries its test or is explicitly marked untested.
- **A test must be capable of failing.** A check that cannot return evidence against the thing it
  checks is not a test (the classification's own rule).
- **Anything specified in order to be tested is `derived = false` until a test passes.**

**TEST ATTACHED TO THIS DECISION:** it fails if any artifact is found reporting as DERIVED an object
that was specified under clause 3(b) in order to be tested. **Grep-auditable; goes on the next register
sweep alongside the Q-48 fence audit.**

---

## 5. WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

**It does not compute alpha and does not move `0 of 18` or `0 of 11`.** It changes what may be
attempted, not what has been achieved. **It authorizes no claim whatever.**

It also does not retract any refutation. Everything killed today — OBS-05, OBS-06 as structural,
theory 001's section 4 — stays killed.
