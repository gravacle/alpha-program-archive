## THE QUESTION

## THE RULING

*** A FINITE HILBERT-SCHMIDT CEILING WAS NOT THE RIGHT OBLIGATION. ***

Ground of record, the principal's, NOT a sealed physical principle: with
`K_bare_zero_adopted_as_compositeness_condition = true` there is no bare term and therefore no
counterterm available by construction to absorb a divergence in the induced coupling. A rule
demanding that the object be BOUNDED therefore demands that the theory not do the thing the
theory says it does. THE OBJECT TO EXTRACT IS THE COEFFICIENT, NOT A CEILING.

CONSISTENCY OF RECORD: this is the same inference the principal already made once, at R-19,
striking the §Q2-STOP divergence-stop AT ITS GENERAL CLAUSE — "a divergence in an induced
coupling with no counterterm available by construction IS the coupling, and a rule halting on
it forbids the measurement the theory requires." R-19 applied it to the TRIPWIRE. This applies
it to the OBLIGATION. The two rulings are one position applied at two places.

CONSEQUENTLY: R-L2b as written is SUPERSEDED / RE-POSED, NOT SOLVED and NOT MERELY REFUTED.
Its refutation under H1 stands as a fact about the obligation AS WRITTEN and is not disturbed;
what changes is that the obligation as written is no longer the thing the program is trying to
discharge.
