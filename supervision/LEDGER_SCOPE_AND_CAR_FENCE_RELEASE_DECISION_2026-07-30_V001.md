the unit does not certify the premise universe exhaustive. **17 of 26 UNTESTED (Q-45)

## 2. CAR-SOURCE FENCE — READ-ONLY RELEASE, CITATION PERMITTED, NO IMPORT

The CAR-named historical floor/mass source, barred from direct lane reading at
`STAGE8_STANDING_PREMISE_TEST_CLASSIFICATION_V001.md:796-799`, is released under a
written condition:

```text
CAR_SOURCE_READ = PERMITTED
CAR_SOURCE_CITE = PERMITTED
CAR_SOURCE_IMPORT_INTO_CONSTRUCTION = FORBIDDEN
CAR_SOURCE_USE_AS_PREMISE_OR_LICENCE = FORBIDDEN
SCOPE = establishing what the source SAYS, for classification and reconciliation only
```

**RATIONALE.** The fences ban ENTRY, not comparison. Reading a historical artifact to
establish what status it records is neither entry nor construction: nothing is
imported, nothing is derived from it, and no premise is licensed by it. **This was the
standing defect — the fence was erected without a release condition, so a lane could
neither use the source nor establish that it was not needed.** A fence with no written
release is not a safeguard; it is an unfalsifiable exclusion.

**WHAT REMAINS FORBIDDEN IS UNCHANGED.** No content of that source may enter a
construction, act as a premise, license a step, or supply a value. A lane that reads it
may report what it says and nothing more.
