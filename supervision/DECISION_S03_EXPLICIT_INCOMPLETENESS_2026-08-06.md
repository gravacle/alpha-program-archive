# DECISION OF RECORD — S03 CARRIED AS EXPLICIT INCOMPLETENESS (2026-08-06)

**Principal ruling (Brian Mulconrey), 2026-08-06.** Slot S03 of the Q-spec —
the dynamical U(1) action — is carried as EXPLICIT INCOMPLETENESS. No
completion is adopted. No authored-residue package is commissioned.

**Basis (cross-reviewed of record, Q-556):** the sealed stock admits an
admissible action family with no sealed selector; the family is not bounded or
finitely enumerated by sealed stock; two concrete completion countermodels
differ in their response; "declaring one minimal is not a derivation"
(`COMPLETE_PARENT_ACTION_UNDERDETERMINATION_GATE_V001.md`); Task 19 stands
BLOCKED_AS_CONSTRUCTION. Because the response feeds the coupling, any selected
member would be an authored physics input that directly shapes the derived
number, with no certificate that the selection was not steered by its
downstream effect. C7 bars selection from the response end; this ruling bars
selection from the construction end until the record itself forces one.

**Rationale in the program's terms:** couplings and the objects that produce
them are OUTPUTS of self-consistency, not stock contents. Where the sealed
record does not determine an object, the lawful state is the record's
non-determination carried openly — the same fallback invoked for ExtSrc —
not an adoption from an unclassified family whose members differ in the
physics.

**Scope:** S03 remains TYPE-U. S04–S08 inherit the block and remain TYPE-U on
the object side. The Q-spec DEMAND side (Q-554/Q-556, corrected V002 pending)
is unaffected. Task 6's critical path (the evaluator, custody, seal rail,
demand-side document) does not depend on S03 and proceeds.

**Release condition (written now, per standing rule):** this incompleteness is
re-opened only by (a) a sealed derivation that bounds or classifies the
mutation family from record structure alone, (b) Task 7's external-realization
junction work supplying a selector or a bounding principle, or (c) a principal
re-ruling of record. Any future adoption must arrive through the
authored-residue discipline with the family first bounded.
