## Collapse Argument

Candidate collapse claim:

"Binds the internal sector" might just mean "the theorem quantifies only over internal objects";
therefore sector reach is merely a special case of quantifier reach.

Determination:

`sector_axis_is_quantifier_axis = false | TYPE-R | test: the two corpus cross-cases above realize
the off-diagonal possibilities.`

Reason:

Quantifier scope answers "for how many objects in a chosen domain does this claim hold?"
Sector reach answers "what is the chosen domain and codomain, and what consumers can receive the
result?"

Those are different questions. A theorem can quantify over all objects in an internal domain and
still have no external-sector output. Conversely, a theorem can mention and preserve source,
record, and field data while being conditional on one fixed action, fixed carrier, fixed quotient,
and fixed regulator/order data.

Thus the collapse depends on equivocation: "over the family" and "over a sector" use the same English
preposition but not the same mathematical role.
