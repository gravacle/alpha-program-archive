## Split

The object called "allow/require" must be split into two differently typed
objects.

1. The allow/require constraint: real, argued, falsifiable, and already run
   against the program's own preferred result. It constrains when declared support
   can become a durable public record. It is not, by itself, an absolute
   stiffness selector.
2. The local minimality or saturation adoptions named after allow/require. These
   are local equality, least-member, or single-normalization moves. They are not

   vocabulary.
