## §1 — Q1: SIGNATURE CLASSIFICATION

**THE CLASSIFICATION RULE, FIXED BEFORE IT IS APPLIED** (the commission's, with
one refinement of mine declared at CH-1):

```text
MODAL        the defining text's output is a verdict, a label, a membership
             fact, or a partition of pre-existing things into forced/permitted
             (admissible/inadmissible).  It returns no object that was not
             already among its inputs.
GENERATIVE   the defining text's output is an object not among its inputs.
MIXED        the defining text does both, in separable clauses, each displayed.
NAMED-ONLY   the item is named and never defined; its signature is not at bytes.
INDETERMINATE-AT-BYTES
             the defining text exists but does not fix which of the two it is.

CH-1 REFINEMENT (mine, declared): a definition whose codomain is a SCALAR
MEASUREMENT of pre-existing input — a norm, a degree, a count, a bound — is
graded **MODAL(measurement)**.  It is a sub-kind of MODAL, not a fourth
category.  The alternative (grading a norm GENERATIVE, since a real number is
strictly "an object not among its inputs") is displayed at CH-1 and rejected
there with its reason.  Every row so graded is marked, so a reader who prefers
the other convention can re-add them: they are CS-3, CS-4, CS-8, F1-a, F3-c.
```
