## 4. The exact O1 bite

### 4.1 Why the record proof works

For a completed-record observable `A in R_N`, the GNS result supplies

```text
W_M = V_(M,N)(W_N tensor I_new),
[V_(M,N), I_source tensor iota_NM(A)] = 0.
```

The later factor can therefore be commuted away inside the expectation, which
gives exact compatibility

```text
omega_M(iota_NM(A))=omega_N(A).
```

That equality produces the record-limit state and then its GNS triple.

### 4.2 Why the same cancellation is unavailable on the full algebra

For a source-inclusive `X in A_N`, embed it as `J_NM(X)`. The same factorization
gives

```text
varpi_M(J_NM(X))
 = omega_in,M(
     (W_N^* tensor I_new)
     V_(M,N)^* J_NM(X) V_(M,N)
     (W_N tensor I_new)).
```

The would-be compatibility defect is therefore

```text
Delta_(M,N)(X)
 = varpi_M(J_NM(X))-varpi_N(X)
 = omega_in,M(
     (W_N^* tensor I_new)
     [V_(M,N)^*J_NM(X)V_(M,N)-J_NM(X)]
     (W_N tensor I_new)),
```

with the natural incoming-state restriction understood. For record-only `X`,
the bracket vanishes by the sealed theorem. On the source factor, later cells
continue to act on the shared source and the required commutator is known not
to vanish in general. The record cancellation cannot be reused.

This is the exact bite point. It does **not** prove that the net of finite
source states diverges. It proves that the exact compatibility engine is gone
and that convergence needs a new estimate or structural theorem.
