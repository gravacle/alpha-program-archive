The capacity principle defines:

```text

```

Source: `alpha_first_durable_record_capacity_principle_v001.md:7-12`.

It then imposes:

```text

N_BR(k_R) = 1,
lambda_0(D_BR^2) = k_R^2,
lambda_1(D_BR^2) > k_R^2.
```

Source: `alpha_first_durable_record_capacity_principle_v001.md:14-22`.

And it gives the trace discipline:

```text

unquotiented Hilbert space, the spectral-projector trace counts multiplicity.
Counting only distinct eigenvalues is not a linear trace...
```

with:

```text

independently constructs a quotient Hilbert space whose lowest public
eigenspace is one-dimensional. Degeneracies may not be discarded merely to make
N_BR(k_R)=1.
```

Source: `alpha_first_durable_record_capacity_principle_v001.md:28-42`.

Determination:

```text

```

quotient are derived, the threshold projector must count exactly one public

the `lambda_1` condition.

But it is not itself the spectral theorem:
