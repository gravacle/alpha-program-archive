tensor carrier, with its finite completed-transfer evolution giving a reversible
unitary write.

public record's three inseparable parts include:

```text

```

thresholded source nonreturn plus exact completed-record persistence, with the


Not as a condition on the finite reversible write.

source tail and completed-record persistence, not from a write-strength
equation. `FORK_8_CAUSAL_DIRECT_LIMIT_PROMOTION_RESULT_V001.md:78-98` states
that for every admitted finite-energy `L2` source root, the spectral theorem
and Riemann-Lebesgue theorem give:

```text

```

```text

```

The same block states that completed-record restrictions are invariant under
later cells, and that source-root nonreturn and public record persistence remain
distinct.


The active `Gamma_K` route requires a `K`-indexed closure operator and scalar
