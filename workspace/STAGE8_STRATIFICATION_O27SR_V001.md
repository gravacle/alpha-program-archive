## §4 — DELIVERABLE 4: THE UNCROSSED BOUNDARY

### §4.1 WHAT KIND OF BOUNDARY IT IS

```text
IT IS NOT AN INDEX BOUNDARY.  Both sides are indexed by the same n.  FP-1 is
"per-composite, all n"; FP-S's quarters are "tr H_A^Sigma_n = 0 IDENTICALLY per
(n, cell), every n".  Same n on both sides.

IT IS NOT A TYPE BOUNDARY.  Both sides evaluate THE SAME FOUR QUANTITIES —
tr K_H, tr H_A, ||K_H||_2^2, ||H_A||_2^2 — at the same trace/HS grade.

*** IT IS A QUANTIFIER-ORDER BOUNDARY. ***  The corpus says so in its own words,
verbatim (COMPLETION_MAP §1, carrying the WSR flag) [2H]:
  "the summed level decides SUM-THEN-QUANTIFY and provably not quantify-then-sum
   ... the V-2-barred bridge is not crossed in either direction; the one-way
   quantifier record (0a10c030) is respected"
The two sides differ in the ORDER of two operations — summation and quantification —
applied to the same objects at the same index.  There are exactly two values and no
more; COMPLETION_MAP §5 CH-2, verbatim: "no third quantifier exists at the consumed
bytes (the one-way record 0a10c030)."

CONSEQUENCE, and it is the point:  A LIFT, A DESCENT, A REFINEMENT, AN INCLUSION AND
A SUMMATION ARE ALL OPERATIONS ON INDICES OR ON TYPES.  NONE OF THEM IS AN OPERATION
ON QUANTIFIER ORDER.  The five bridge kinds this commission was told to look for are
the wrong shape for this boundary — not absent from it, but inapplicable to it.  That
is why the corpus can cross the per-stratum -> stratum-sum boundary (MM-1c, a
summation, derived) and cannot cross this one with any object of the same family.
```

      "The summed decisions transport NOTHING down to the per-composite failure

      "the V-2-barred bridge is not crossed in either direction; the one-way

       'the V-2-barred bridge is not crossed in either direction; the one-way
