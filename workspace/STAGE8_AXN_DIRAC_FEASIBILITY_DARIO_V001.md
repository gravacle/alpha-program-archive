## 3. The incompatibility, proved at that route [PROVABLE]

**Setting, all from sealed or booked ground.** `B ≅ C(Y)` with `Y` the countable product of circles
(member 04) — so `B` is nontrivial. `i_B(b) = 1_A_src ⊗ 1_R_inf ⊗ b` is the booked history embedding,
an injective unital `*`-homomorphism. The entered field is typed
`scalar_pairing_or_trace: faithful declared pairing with normalization convention`, so the pairing
carries `<X,Y> = Tr_joint(X^dagger Y)` with `Tr_joint` faithful.

**Theorem.** *Let the joint pairing be faithful, and let `omega_hist` be its exact restriction object
along `i_B`, normalized as the instrument's route prescribes. Then `omega_hist` is faithful on `B`.*

*Proof.* Let `b in B`, `b != 0`. Since `i_B` is an injective `*`-homomorphism, `i_B(b) != 0` and
`i_B(b^dagger b) = i_B(b)^dagger i_B(b)`. Therefore

```text
omega_hist(b^dagger b) * <1_A_C0, 1_A_C0>  =  <1_A_C0, i_B(b^dagger b)>
                                           =  Tr_joint( i_B(b)^dagger i_B(b) )
                                           =  <i_B(b), i_B(b)>  >  0
```

the last step by faithfulness of the pairing applied to the nonzero element `i_B(b)`. The
normalization is a positive scalar, so `omega_hist(b^dagger b) > 0` for every `b != 0`. ∎

**Corollary — the demand is unsatisfiable.** A faithful positive functional on `C(Y)` has **full
support**. The identity-supported Dirac control is `omega_epsilon = epsilon_+ ⊗ epsilon_-` with
`epsilon(U_lambda) = 1` for every `lambda` — *"evaluation at the trivial character"* at member 04's
bytes — a **point mass**, whose support is a single point of `Y`. For `Y` the countable circle
product, a point is a proper closed subset. Full support and point support coincide only if `C(Y)` is
trivial, which the record forbids. **So no faithful pairing can induce, through the pinned restriction
route, a functional exactly equivalent to the identity-supported Dirac control.**

**RESTRICTION_ROUTE = INCOMPATIBLE-PROVED.**
