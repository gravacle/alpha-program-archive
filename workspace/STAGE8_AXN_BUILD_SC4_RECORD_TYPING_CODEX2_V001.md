## 4. Dimension adjudication

### 4.1 Finite-dimensionality is not forced

Dimension requires at least a specified vector/module/affine carrier and its
scalar operations. The 844 definition is only

```text
X_prim := { families {L_c}, one interaction density per primitive incidence c,
            with support(L_c) subset Omega_c }.
```

It gives neither addition nor scalar multiplication nor a basis. Even the
phrase `dim(X_prim per cell)` is therefore not yet a well-typed record
quantity.

The tempting finite inference fails in four distinct ways:

```text
finite causal support     != finite-dimensional density carrier;
finite-dimensional fiber != finite-dimensional space of cell sections;
one operator line         != one action-integrand line;
finite number of cells K  != a basis theorem for each L_c.
```

The missing arrow is exactly the build's U1/action-to-receiver variation map.
Using the packet's finite output line to define its own action-domain basis
would reverse that arrow and repeat 845's P1 type error.

### 4.2 Infinite-dimensionality is also not forced

A proof of genuine analytic freedom would need one sealed generator of an
infinite independent family, for example:

```text
a declared function algebra acting on an admitted nonzero density;
a module of sections with infinitely many independent supported members;
or an explicit infinite admissible coefficient family.
```

No such primitive-sector object occurs in the exact `L_c` census. The
continuum causal diamond and the phrase “interaction density” do not by
themselves license arbitrary smooth bump functions. Supplying
`C_c^infinity(Omega_c)`, a Sobolev space, or even closure under multiplication
by arbitrary smooth scalars would be the smooth import the relay asks this
audit to detect.

Nor can the effective/nonprimitive remainder be used as the infinite
generator. 844 declared that remainder **out of scope** for restricted
`X_prim`; CIS merely permits same-parent descendants and supplies no cardinality
theorem for them. Importing their openness into the primitive per-cell
dimension would violate the scope declaration.

Therefore:

```text
FINITE-DIMENSIONAL = NOT PROVED.
INFINITE-DIMENSIONAL = NOT PROVED.
DIMENSION = NOT-FORCED.
FREEDOM LOCATION = primitive L_c carrier is under-typed already;
                   it is not leakage from the excluded effective remainder.
```

## 5. What record-native SC4 actually owes

The class-wide variation cannot be a total map until its domain and admissible
directions are typed. That minimum is record-native because it is demanded by
the build's own `U1 : X -> Y`, not by a smooth comparison target.

A future discharge must derive, without selection:

```text
SC4_NATIVE(c) :=
  one declared carrier A_c for admissible primitive interaction densities;
  a membership statement L_c in A_c;
  the admissible-direction/path relation through each admitted L_c;
  closure sufficient for the class-wide variation U1 to be defined;
  preservation of CIS support, one-use, and new-record-factor typing;
  and a proof that the declaration is complete at the claimed primitive scope.
```

This does **not** preselect the form of `A_c`.

- If sealed stock later derives a finite generator manifest and its
  completeness, ordinary finite-dimensional calculus may discharge SC4 with
  no imported regularity class.
- If it derives a continuum section/function carrier, the needed analytic
  regularity then becomes a genuine consequence of that carrier.
- Today neither branch is forced, and choosing between them is forbidden.

No independent boundedness or nondegeneracy residue is imposed on `L_c` by the
sources checked. The packet's bounded perturbation and common self-adjoint
domain are receiver-side tests. SC5's closure-face/boundary prescription also
remains a separate TYPE-U item; this typing neither discharges nor rewrites it.
