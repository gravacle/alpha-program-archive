## §5 — Q5: THE SHAPE OF THE GAP

*Report only. No object is authored, drafted, sketched, or named as it should read.*

Something does compose — three joins are performed of record — so the gap is not
that nothing composes; it is that **the one join the machinery needs is the one
that never happens, and the class of thing missing is a supply, not a licence.**

**intermediate object of dilation type** and an **admissibility proof of
identification type**. The first would have to range over the two-factor

imported") which is a refusal rather than a theorem, and holds the antecedent of

are downstream of one absence the corpus names for itself — the complete
parameter-free microscopic parent — and neither is closed by a coercion between
types already displayed: the types on the two sides of each hole are not near-misses
