## What The Schur Step Establishes If Its Hypotheses Hold

```text

```

is unique up to one common scale.

Inside that full `su(5)`-invariant symmetric-tensor class, the ratio-protection
claim survives: an additive deformation constrained to that class is
proportional to the same parent trace form, so it cannot alter the displayed
Cartan inner-product ratios.

## Verdict

`CONDITIONAL-ON-WHAT`.

The Schur protection survives as a conditional algebraic statement:

```text
IF every admissible additive deformation and every response-changing mutation
that reaches the Cartan Hessian is constrained to a full su(5)-invariant
symmetric 2-tensor on the same transported parent carrier, with no independent
per-factor threshold/local corrections below the breaking scale, THEN the
Cartan ratios are protected up to one common scalar.
```

The corpus does not establish that antecedent.  It establishes conditional
`su(5)` algebra, adopted 3+2 parent structure, and branch-local exclusion of
primitive Pauli insertions; it does not prove that all admissible counterterms
or generated effective descendants respect full `su(5)` rather than the
unbroken twelve-dimensional algebra, nor that transport below the breaking
scale forbids independent per-factor threshold corrections.

This does not refute Schur's lemma.  It refutes using the Schur step as an
unconditional protection theorem for all response-changing deformations in the
current corpus.
