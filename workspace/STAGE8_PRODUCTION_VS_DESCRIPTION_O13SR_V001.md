## §1 — DELIVERABLE 1: THE PRODUCTION SWEEP

### §1.1 The distinction, fixed before it is applied

```text
(a) DYNAMICS WITHIN A RECORD.  A structure that evolves, propagates, or
    transports CONTENT on a structure that already exists.  Test: does the
    object's DOMAIN presuppose the carrier?  If the rule takes a state (or a
    section, or an operator) on an already-given carrier and returns another
    such, it is (a) — however much it is described as "forming" something.

(b) PRODUCTION OF A RECORD.  A structure whose OUTPUT is that there is a record
    at all: a generation rule with makings in its domain, a production map, a
    construction process, or an admissibility criterion quantifying over
    MAKINGS rather than over MADE OBJECTS.  Test: is there anything the rule
    ranges over, other than states of a carrier the rule did not make?

THE TEST IS APPLIED TO DOMAINS, NOT TO VERBS.  The corpus is full of the verbs
("forms", "writes", "generates", "creates", "opens").  This commission reads
what each verb's rule actually takes in.
```

### §2.1 The axis, and a DISCRIMINATOR derived from the commission's own two lists

The commission fixes the axis by exemplars. I do not classify by feel; I extract a
mechanical discriminator from those exemplars and then apply it to bytes.

```text
THE COMMISSION'S OWN EXEMPLARS
  STATE   — an order relation between norms; a dimension; a count; an inclusion.
  PROCESS — a rate per something; a flux; an evaluation performed on an oriented
            cell; a per-unit quantity.

THE DISCRIMINATOR, read off those four-and-four.  What separates the two lists is
ORIENTATION PARITY — whether the item is defined relative to a DIRECTION OF
PASSAGE, so that reversing the passage changes it:
  (P-i)  ORIENTATION-SENSITIVE.  The definition contains a direction of crossing
         (source -> target; in -> out; a chosen normal; a chain and its reverse),
         and reversing it changes the item's value by a sign, or changes which
         object is being evaluated.
  (P-ii) PER-UNIT.  The item is a quotient by an extensive parameter — "per
         something" — so its value depends on a division of the object into units
         THROUGH WHICH the quantity passes.
  EITHER clause suffices for PROCESS.  NEITHER => STATE.

  a norm inequality   ||-A|| = ||A||          — no orientation, no denominator  => STATE  [CAS S1b]

  an evaluation on an oriented cell  Phi(delta_{e^-}) = -Phi(delta_e)           => PROCESS  [CAS S2a]
  a per-unit quantity a value carried by a UNIT symbol                          => PROCESS
  a rate per something   a quotient by an extensive parameter                   => PROCESS
ALL EIGHT EXEMPLARS ARE CLASSIFIED CORRECTLY BY THE DISCRIMINATOR.  It is used
below with no further adjustment.
```

### §3.1 FINDING 1 — the native invariants are localized and scale-free but carry NO MAGNITUDE

**VERDICT: EXPLAINS — and the mechanism is exhibited, not asserted.**

```text
THE FINDING, at its own bytes (O10SR §3.2/§5.3):
  "EVERY row that is SCALE-FREE AND LOCAL carries NO MAGNITUDE.  THE ONE row that
   carries a MAGNITUDE AND IS LOCAL is NOT SCALE-FREE.  There is no row with all
   three."  and  "scale-freedom and magnitude-carriage are, across this inventory,
   in exact opposition."

THE MECHANISM THE SHAPE TEST SUPPLIES:
 (i)  §2.3 derives that EVERY scale-free-and-local native row is PROCESS-typed —
      N-1, N-3, N-4 fully; N-2 and N-5d with localization PARTIAL.  Their
      PROCESS-hood is orientation-oddness (CAS S2a/S2b, S5b).
 (ii) A MAGNITUDE is an orientation-BLIND non-negative scalar: it must survive
      being read off either orientation, since a demand of the form Q <= M carries
      no orientation to fix a sign with.
 (iii) BRIDGE THEOREM [CAS S3a]: an orientation-odd functional's orientation-blind
      (symmetric) part is IDENTICALLY ZERO.  (1/2)[Phi(c) + Phi(-c)] = 0 exactly,
      under the record's own CE-2c.
 (iv) THEREFORE the orientation-blind content extractable from a PROCESS-typed
      native asset is only what survives the erasure |.| — and the record's own
      values show exactly what that is, row by row:
        N-1  entries in {-1,0,+1}      -> erasure gives {0,1}: a table of BITS.
        N-3  identically 0 on the exact class [CAS S5e] -> erasure gives 0.
        N-4  value set the sign group  -> erasure gives 1.  A pure number.
        N-2/N-5d  a DIMENSION          -> already even; a count, not a size.
      NONE OF THESE IS A SIZE.  And [CAS S3c] a certificate whose value is 0
      majorizes nothing positive — the relation m <= 0 is unsatisfiable for m > 0.
 (v)  AND THE ONE EXCEPTION IS EXPLAINED BY THE SAME AXIS, NOT AGAINST IT.  N-4'
      is the only native row carrying a magnitude, and §2.3 types it PROCESS
      **doubly** — orientation-odd AND per-unit.  Its magnitude arrives through
      clause (P-ii): `lambda_JD` is "pulled outside the line integral AS A UNIT".
      The magnitude is a PER-UNIT quantity; that is what a unit symbol IS.
*** SO THE "NO MAGNITUDE" FINDING IS NOT A CONTINGENCY OF THE INVENTORY.  IT IS
    WHAT PROCESS-TYPING ENTAILS: an orientation-odd object's orientation-blind
    residue is 0, a sign, or a count — and a magnitude appears in exactly one row,
    in the same line that introduces its unit. ***
STRENGTH DECLARED: this is a mechanism displayed over the commissioned inventory,
  not a theorem about all possible types.  Stated at that strength and no higher —
  the same strength O10SR itself declared for the pattern.
```

## §4 — DELIVERABLE 4: WHY THE GENERATIVE FORCING NOTION IS ABSENT

The commission names two situations with different repairs. They are separated here
at bytes.

```text
SITUATION 1 — UNSTATABLE.  The notion is absent BECAUSE the production it would
  quantify over is unrepresented.  The quantifier has no domain of discourse.
  REPAIR: representing the production is a PREREQUISITE; building the notion is
  not the repair and cannot be attempted first.
SITUATION 2 — UNBUILT.  A production IS represented; the notion over it simply has
  not been written.  REPAIR: write the notion; the furniture is already there.
```

### §4.1 What the notion would need, decomposed

```text
The absent predicate, as O12SR §3.2 names its search target (named, NOT proposed
and NOT adopted): "invariant across every lawful generation/production of a
record".  A quantifier of that form needs THREE things:
  (D)  A DOMAIN — a set/class/category of lawful productions to range over.
  (E)  AN EVALUATION — a map from a production to the record it produces, so that
       "the quantity of THIS production's record" is defined.
  (I)  AN INVARIANCE — the claim that the quantity agrees across the domain.
```

### §4.2 Each of the three, tested against both roots

```text
(D) THE DOMAIN.  SWEEP P3, uncapped, both roots: *** ZERO HITS. ***  No set,
    class, category, space, moduli, or indexed family of record productions
    exists anywhere in either root (§1.5).  There is nothing to range over.
```
