## Flat outgoing-tail continuum

On the ordinary flat `3+1` branch with future unit normal, use the spatial
Cauchy carrier

```text
H=L2(R^3; C direct-sum C^3).
```

The scalar component is the continuum `0`-cochain and the vector component
is the continuum `1`-cochain. On the scalar-plus-exact-one-form invariant
sector define

```text
D psi=grad psi,
H_0=[[0,D^dagger],[D,0]].
```

In Fourier space,

```text
d(k)=i k,
h(k)=[[0,d(k)^dagger],[d(k),0]],
h(k)^2=|k|^2
```

on the scalar-longitudinal sector. The evaluator must prove self-adjointness
on the maximal Fourier-multiplier domain and identify the positive-energy
projector. Transverse zero modes may not be discarded; their overlap with
the declared scalar root must be computed.

## Cellular refinement

For spacing `a>0`, use the geometrically normalized cellular difference

```text
d_a,j(k)=[exp(i a k_j)-1]/a
```

on the Brillouin zone `[-pi/a,pi/a]^3`, and its self-adjoint block dilation
`h_a(k)`. The factor `1/a` is the coframe conversion from a dimensionless
incidence difference to a physical derivative. It is not a response
normalization.

Extend the bounded multiplication operator by zero outside its Brillouin
zone. Prove:

```text
h_a(k)->h(k) pointwise;
(H_a-z)^(-1)->(H_0-z)^(-1) strongly for Im(z) != 0;
exp(-itH_a)->exp(-itH_0) strongly for fixed t.
```

The proof must use resolvent domination; it may not invoke the prior
uniformly bounded direct-limit theorem, because `||H_a||` diverges as
`a->0`.

## Root-state map

The future normal selects the unique maximal-volume waist of the unit causal
diamond. Let `B` be its radius-`1/2` spatial ball and

```text
psi_B(x)=1_B(x)/sqrt(Vol_3(B))
```

in the scalar component.

The evaluator must:

1. compute its normalized Fourier transform;
2. prove that its radial form factor equals the full diamond null-shell form
   factor from R3.4 v002;
3. compute its overlap with the positive, negative, and transverse spectral
   projectors; and
4. derive the normalized positive-energy root spectral measure from
   `<psi_B,P_+(dE)psi_B>`, including Fourier and angular factors.

No density may be inserted by hand.
