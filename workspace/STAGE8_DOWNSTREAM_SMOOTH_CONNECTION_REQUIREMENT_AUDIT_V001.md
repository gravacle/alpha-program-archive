1. the Gate-4 unit-weight incidence differential modulo vertex gauge;
2. finite edge transport, loop/face holonomy, and formal face curvature;
3. the finite charged-handle and flux-conditioned write algebras;
4. the onset-saturation test; and
5. the finite graph Ward identity and finite response diagnostics, within
   their stated scopes.

It forfeits, under the current specifications:

1. the Step-1 result `physical_public_EM_connection_derived`;
2. a local exterior derivative and connection one-form whose curvature is the
   physical spacetime `F_mu_nu`;

   amplitude and retarded Hessian;

normalization path. This is not a no-go theorem for discrete physics. It is a
statement that the corpus has not supplied the equivalence theorem needed to
