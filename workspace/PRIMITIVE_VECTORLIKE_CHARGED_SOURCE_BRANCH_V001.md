## Lorentz and CPT completion

In `3+1` dimensions, a local charged spinor decomposes into left- and
right-handed Weyl representations. A chiral `U(1)` spectrum must satisfy both
the cubic and mixed gravitational anomaly conditions:

```text
sum_i q_i^3 = 0,
sum_i q_i = 0.
```

One nonzero unit-charge Weyl field alone fails both conditions. The smallest
vectorlike completion adds its opposite-charge conjugate. Written as
left-handed fields, the charges are

```text
(+1, -1).
```

Equivalently, they form one four-component Dirac field with a unit vector
charge. Spin-statistics makes this carrier fermionic.

## Multiplicity ceiling

One vectorlike pair is the minimal anomaly-free realization of the declared
primitive charged spinor branch. Additional vectorlike pairs are consistent
theories and are not proved impossible. They introduce extra fundamental
flavor multiplicity and change the charged response.

Composite durable excitations are not counted as independent microscopic
flavors unless the sealed action derives them as independent ultraviolet
