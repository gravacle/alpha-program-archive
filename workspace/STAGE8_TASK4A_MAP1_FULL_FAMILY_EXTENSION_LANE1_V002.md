## 6. E3 -- exact higher-rank obstruction for one terminal scalar

### 6.1 Classification of scalar-character lifts

Let `rank C_G=b`. By DoR-015,

```text
       lambda in Hom(C_G,Z) }.
```

member gives the trivial lift. No member is selected here.

### 6.3 No-selection automorphism obstruction

Let `sigma` be the admitted realization automorphism that exchanges the two
parallel edges `a` and `d` and fixes `b` and both vertices. It acts on the
cycle lattice by

```text
sigma(c_1)=c_2,
sigma(c_2)=c_1,
sigma(c_3)=-c_3.                                  (E3-3)
```

The consumed scalar `Z_N` is a product of relative-history characters. It is
unchanged by this relabeling. A full-family extension with no realization

```text

```

Applying that identity to `(E3-3)` gives

```text

lambda(c_3)
 =lambda(c_1-c_2)
 =0.                                               (E3-4)
```

Consequently every no-selection scalar lift has trivial holonomy on the


separation theorem makes it record-visible quotient content. Thus a
family-natural lift necessarily deletes a record-visible direction. A lift
with `lambda(c_3)!=0` avoids that deletion only by distinguishing `a` from
`d`; that is exactly an unratified realization/edge-direction selection.

This proof uses only the target cycle carrier and its admitted automorphism.
It does not assume the unbuilt relative-label-to-edge realization functor.
