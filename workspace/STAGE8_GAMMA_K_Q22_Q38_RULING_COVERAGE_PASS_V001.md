## Coverage-Detector Recommendation

The semantic question "does row Q-n bear on artifact A" is not fully
mechanical. A `corpus_check.py` detector that tried to decide bearing would be
checker-shaped overreach.

A narrower detector is well-posed:

```text
register_coverage_through = Q-n
register_rows_considered = Q-a..Q-n
known_bearing_rows = [...]
```

For any artifact that cites a register row or declares a construction/test
target, `corpus_check.py` can report YELLOW when:

1. the artifact lacks a `register_coverage_through` declaration;
2. the register head is greater than the artifact's declared coverage row; or
3. the artifact cites Q-m while a later coverage artifact names a bearing row
   Q-k > Q-m that the artifact does not cite.

That detector would not decide substance. It would force a coverage pass before
execution. It would have caught the present failure mode: V001 cited Q-22 while
the register head had advanced to Q-38, and Amendment 001 cited no Q-row at all.
