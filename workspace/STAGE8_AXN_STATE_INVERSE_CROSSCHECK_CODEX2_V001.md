## 6. EVENNESS AND THE SPATIAL IMPLEMENTER — THEOREM CONFIRMED, APPLICATION CORRECTED

Let `A` be one C-star algebra, let `alpha:A->A` be an involutive star-automorphism, and let `omega`
be a state on **that same algebra** with `omega o alpha = omega`. In the GNS representation define on
the dense cyclic subspace:

```text
U_0 pi_omega(a)Omega_omega := pi_omega(alpha(a))Omega_omega.
```

Then

```text
||U_0 pi(a)Omega||^2
  = omega(alpha(a)^* alpha(a))
  = omega(alpha(a^*a))
  = omega(a^*a).
```

So `U_0` is well-defined and isometric, extends to a unitary `U`, and satisfies

```text
U pi(a) U^* = pi(alpha(a)).
```

Because `alpha^2=id`, `U^2=I` on a dense set and hence everywhere. A unitary involution obeys
`U=U^*`. Thus evenness is sufficient for a self-adjoint spatial implementer. Quasi-equivalence of
`omega` and `omega o alpha` is a necessary representation-class condition; it is not asserted here
as the same sufficient cyclic-vector construction.

The theorem is sound. Its application in 894 omitted the load-bearing same-algebra binding. The
sealed `alpha_infinity` is an algebraic-limit automorphism, the covector state is displayed on an
`M_2` inductive system, and the outgoing-record GNS is displayed on an `M_3` inductive system. Until
a sealed map places `alpha_infinity` on the exact algebra represented by the chosen state, no
`Gamma_infinity` for that candidate has been constructed. The final ruling is therefore
**CORRECTED**, not a rejection of the involution proof.
