refinement) and my 862 re-derivation against the grading both establish that **no sealed even-sector
splitter exists**. So a skeleton assigning separate weights to `h²` and `W²` would be splitting an
unsplittable block. **Composition removes that apparent freedom.**

variation — the perturbation must be **record-odd**:


record factor only); **NF-3 constrains its parity**, and it arises only when the census meets the
