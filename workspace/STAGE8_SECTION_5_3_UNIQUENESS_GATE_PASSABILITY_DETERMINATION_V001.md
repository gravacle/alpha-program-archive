## 1. Is Section 5.3's Admitted Family The Allowance Envelope?

No.

simple positive root, no inequivalent admitted positive root, and no continuous
modulus (`STAGE8_GAMMA_K_CONSTRUCTION_SPEC_V001.md:575-589`). The source
selector says the strict route is authorized only if `C_record(K_*) = 0`,
`d C_record / dK at K_* != 0`, `K_* > 0`, and an exhaustive admitted-family
audit finds no other inequivalent positive root
(`primitive_record_cell_selection_principle_v002.md:91-109`).

The preregistration names the family audited before root solving as admitted
geometry, clock, measure, regulator, and action-partition alternatives
(`primitive_record_cell_selection_preregistration_v002.json:15-23, 36-43`).
V001 restates the same five channels
(`STAGE8_GAMMA_K_CONSTRUCTION_SPEC_V001.md:530-559`).

theory output after withdrawing the eight clothed adoptions, not the root

(`STAGE8_ALLOWANCE_ENVELOPE_WITH_CLOTHING_WITHDRAWN_V001.md:48-78, 224-234`).
It includes several axes that Section 5.3 does not itself range over as a
mutation channel, such as record grading and sector functor
(`STAGE8_ALLOWANCE_ENVELOPE_WITH_CLOTHING_WITHDRAWN_V001.md:87-96, 110-135`).

## 3. What Would Make Section 5.3 Passable?

Three candidates were assessed.

### Candidate A: Closure Theorem Bounding The Action-Form Class

This is the live satisfaction route.

It would have to prove, before response/root evaluation, that every
target-independent action-partition/action-form mutation is either:

1. generated by a finite or otherwise exhaustively specified class;
2. physically equivalent under a derived equivalence relation; or
3. excluded by an upstream principle that does not inspect the root or response
   value.

This would not soften Section 5.3. It would supply the object that makes
"exhaustive admitted-family audit" meaningful.

A narrower reading could make the gate passable only if sealed text already
defined the admitted family more narrowly than the full allowance envelope
while still covering all five preregistered mutation channels. That narrower
reading exists only in one sense: Section 5.3 is narrower than the whole
allowance envelope. It does not avoid the action-form channel.

Therefore a reading that narrows Section 5.3 away from action-partition /
action-form mutations would be an amendment or supersession, not an execution
of current Section 5.3.
