## §7 — THE Z.2 CHECK, REPORTED AND NOT REWRITTEN

The spec makes the Z.2 (a)/(b)/(c) declaration MANDATORY alongside a nonzero
certification. It was written for the failure reading. **Read under the new
typing, it still parses — and its substance is not merely compatible with the
ruling but anticipates it.** `§Z.2` at `:1530-1552` opens:

> **"A DIVERGENT MAJORANT IS NOT A DIVERGENT THEORY."**

**THAT IS THE RULING'S OWN DISTINCTION, ALREADY SEALED IN THE SPEC**, drawn there
at the level of the bound and drawn by the principal at the level of the
coupling. Z.2 is a guard AGAINST over-reading divergence as pathology; the new
typing runs with it, not against it. And option **(c)** — *"only that the CURRENT
architecture's bound fails — which EXCLUDES NOTHING and licenses only further IR
work"* — is a coherent landing for a nonzero-as-induced-coupling result.

**ONE SEAM, REPORTED BECAUSE IT IS REAL AND NARROW:**

```text
Z.2's HEADER makes the declaration "MANDATORY on any certified-divergent arm;
  BLOCK on omission" — it fires on the ARM.
Z.2's BODY addresses "Any artifact of this program that would EXCLUDE the sharp
  localizer" — it fires on an EXCLUSION.
UNDER THE FAILURE READING THESE COINCIDED: a certified-divergent arm WAS an
  exclusion, so header and body picked out the same case.
UNDER THE NEW TYPING THEY COME APART: a nonzero arm still trips the header,
  while the body's (a)/(b)/(c) menu is framed for an artifact seeking to
  EXCLUDE the sharp localizer — which, on the new typing, it is not.
```

```text
VERDICT: Z.2 PARSES AND IS NOT CONTRADICTED. NO ERRATUM IS REQUIRED FOR
  INCOHERENCE. The seam is a scope question — whether the mandatory declaration
  is owed by an arm that excludes nothing — and it is the principal's, not this
  lane's. REPORTED, NOT REWRITTEN, PER THE CHARTER.
```
