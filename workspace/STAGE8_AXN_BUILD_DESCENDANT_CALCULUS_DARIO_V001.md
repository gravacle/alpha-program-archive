## 1. AS1 — THE GENERATION RULES OF RECORD

### 1.1 G1 — descendants are SQUARE-GENERATED, and carry no independent coefficient

R3 packet copy `345d447eaf6d730c`. Its verdict block lists among the parent's content
*"all **square-generated** descendants"*, and `[1066,1673)` sha `6e880eadfd1682b8`:

> *"For the finite Galerkin regression, **direct squaring of the parent agrees with the declared
> generated decomposition**: full square identity error = 0; overlap-descendant norm = 16;
> derivative-support descendant norm = 9.797958971132712; **independent descendant coefficient used
> = false**."*

and `[1752,1971)` sha `5f8c230f6a3d953d`:

> *"Thus the overlap and closure-face terms **are generated by the parent. They were not added as
> separate dynamics.**"*

**RULE G1.** Given a candidate first-order presentation `D`, its descendant set is whatever `D²`
produces under the declared decomposition. **No descendant may carry an independently supplied
coefficient**, and no descendant may be *added*. The sealed instance exhibits two descendant kinds —
overlap and derivative-support (equivalently closure-face) — with displayed norms.

This is the calculus's engine and it is **selection-free by construction**: squaring is a total
operation on a presentation, so it makes no choices.
