## 5. Mandatory kill-pass: realization or relabeling?

### 5.1 The criterion comes from sealed P5 text

`STAGE8_P5_COMMON_ORIGIN_DESCENT_REQUIREMENT_V001.md:66-99` requires one
microscopic construction trace or source realization `Omega`, typed maps to
state, effects, domains, and dynamics, and a provenance certificate that the
outputs share the same primitive inputs and construction record.

Its `:127-150` permits a noninjective descent—so unique full-origin
determination is unnecessary—but immediately states:

> `This does not reduce common origin to a bare existential assertion. A
> concrete construction trace and commuting descent data are required. "Some
> source exists" or "all fields appear in one file" is insufficient.`

The current Piece-1 contract makes the operation explicit at
`STAGE8_P5_COMMON_ORIGIN_DESCENT_PIECE1_ORIGIN_TRACE_ATTEMPT_V001.md:122-157`:

> `Gen_Omega is load-bearing. It is the operation, evolution law, action, or
> other construction rule that produces the outputs from Prim_Omega. A tuple
> that merely contains C0_008, rho_pre, {E_r}, and the domains does not inhabit
> this type.`

Q-194 independently states the same separation at
`STAGE8_CTP_PHYS_INPUT_PACKAGE_B0_SUBSTITUTE_ADMISSIBILITY_ADJUDICATION_V001.md:313-322`:
a complete certified presentation is extensionally sufficient, but every datum
must be tied to one frozen construction trace through complete descent
witnesses; mere co-location is insufficient.

The criterion is therefore:

```text
REALIZATION:
  outputs are obtained by executable construction operations from antecedent
  primitive data, and the ordered trace proves their common provenance.

RELABELING:
  outputs are already independent coordinates of the alleged origin and the
  proposed descent maps merely project those coordinates.
```
