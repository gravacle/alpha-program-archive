## 1. The property

Let `A_HD` be the algebra of admitted local higher-derivative action mutations before any root exists, and
let

```text
Resp : A_HD -> I_R
```

denote the structural response functor into the invariants that the completed residual can consume. This is
a type-level map; it is not evaluated here.

A microscopic generator fixes the tower in response-relevant content iff it supplies all four parts of a
**RESPONSE-COMPLETE FINITE PRESENTATION**:

1. **Generators:** a finite set `G={g_1,...,g_r}`, or an executable finite-state generation grammar, from
   which every admitted member of `A_HD` is produced.
2. **Relations/kernel:** a target-independent decision procedure for whether two generated words differ by
   gauge/public isometry/orientation/Boundary-Resolved equivalence, EOM redundancy, fixed boundary terms, or
   another derived null response transformation.
3. **Coefficient descent:** an exact rule fixing every generated descendant coefficient from a finite set of
   primitive data. An independent Wilson coefficient at every derivative order fails this condition even if
   the operator words are generated by one symbol.
4. **Response completeness:** a proof that `Resp` factors through the presented quotient and that no admitted
   response-changing mutation lies outside its image.

Equivalently, the response quotient

```text
A_HD / ker(Resp)
```

must possess an executable finite presentation whose coefficients are fixed by finite upstream data. Merely
saying “all terms descend from one operator” is insufficient unless the descent fixes their relative
coefficients and proves no outside response class exists.

This matches the sealed operational rule that a generation rule suffices, while a bound suffices only with an
executable cover and no-outside decision procedure
(`STAGE8_ACTION_FORM_CLOSURE_THEOREM_SPEC_AND_BUILD_STOP_V001.md:240-272`).

## 2. Why this is weaker than the complete origin

The property determines only the image and kernel relevant to physical response. Distinct microscopic origin
objects may share the same response-complete presentation while differing in response-invisible state data,
off-shell coordinatization, auxiliary realization, or other non-consumed structure. Therefore:

```text
response_complete_finite_presentation_implies_full_origin_determination = false | TYPE-R |
  test: quotient construction permits distinct origins with identical Resp image and kernel
```

Full origin determination is sufficient but not necessary. The minimal certificate is:

```text
(G, relations, coefficient-descent rule, response-completeness proof).
```

This uses the sealed “producers consume structure, not bare existence” distinction: the consuming gate needs
the presentation delivered at its attempt ports, not an existence flag or a unique metaphysical realization.

## 5. Comparison with the other closure routes

**Route 1 — finite response-relevant cutoff.** Syntactically cheapest: one derived theorem could state that
operators above order `N` are response-null or exactly redundant. It avoids the full generator. The corpus
currently has no such cutoff, and an EFT suppression estimate would not suffice for an exact uniqueness gate.

**Route 2 — all-orders convergence/resummation.** Also avoids full origin determination if it proves that the
entire tower collapses to finitely many exact response invariants. It is analytically heavier because it needs
uniform convergence, measure/regulator control, and compatibility with the completed response map.

**Route 3 — microscopic generator/presentation.** It can avoid full origin determination by producing only
the response-complete finite presentation. It is attractive because the corpus already has candidate
filtration and quasi-free generation principles, but they are adopted and incomplete.

**Route 4 — stronger native finite-algebra theorem.** This is the cheapest route suggested by existing native
structure: upgrade the incidence filtration from an adopted primitive rule to a derived theorem, then prove
that its represented response quotient is finitely presented and coefficient-complete. It avoids reconstructing
the entire origin, but it must cover continuum/time-dependent, CTP, measure, and response layers rather than
only the finite stationary one-complex.
