forms, `..`, and resolution outside the declared source root. A supplied
carrier instance must pass all of:

1. file SHA-256 and UTF-8/JSON parsing;
2. its sealed box's closed schema (`type`, `required`,
   `additionalProperties`, local `$ref`, `const`, `enum`, `pattern`, bounds,

its SHA-256, write `components/<sha256>.json`, and bind that digest into the
row result. No such branch was taken on the real census because no sealed
instance source is of record.
