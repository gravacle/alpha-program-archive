# DECISION OF RECORD — THE CORPUS-MEMBER SELECTION AUTHORITY (2026-08-08, principal ruling)

The thirteen Step-11 corpus-instance boxes (Q-628) lack a sealed member-selection
authority. The principal rules: RULE + REGISTRAR + DUAL VERIFY.

1. A MECHANICAL SELECTION RULE generates each candidate member set: all sealed
   artifacts in the program space matching the blocker's named scope under the
   FOUR-MODE OBJECT-NAMES PROBE (fixed-string; whitespace-normalized;
   hyphen/apostrophe-variant; object-vocabulary aliases), searched spaces stated.
2. THE REGISTRAR — neutral custodian, never a producer — runs the rule and
   seals each corpus instance (schema per V007's SEALED_CORPUS_DEFINITION;
   members content-addressed).
3. BOTH LANES independently verify set-completeness (each lane hunts for
   wrongly included AND wrongly excluded members) BEFORE any M2 query runs
   against the instance. A lane finding stands as a finding of record.

Rationale: producer selection is BR-1's shape (deciding what counts as
searchable); pure mechanism has no catch for an over/under-inclusive rule;
principal-per-corpus does not scale. Rule-generated, registrar-sealed,
dual-verified selections are regenerable, neutral, and cross-checked.
