## Test 3 - Record Without Source

Question: Can a record exist with no source having acted?

### Evidence

`BID_GLOBAL_CAR_RECORD_COMPOSITION_DERIVATION_V001.md:66-88` defines labeled
record carriers and record algebras before any specific source action:

```text

A_SR(C)=CAR(K_Sigma,q) graded-tensor R(C)
```

The same artifact then says that, because record operators have trivial
fermion grading, the graded tensor product with `R(C)` reduces to the ordinary
tensor product and every record operator commutes with the source algebra.

`COMPLETE_QSPEC_RELATIVE_HISTORY_TRANSFER_MAP_REPAIR_SPEC_V001.md:25-33`
requires fresh record factors and a declared ready state at each sequential
stage:

```text

U_c acts as identity on every closed record factor;
R_c begins in its declared ready state.
```

`BID_CHARGED_HANDLE_ACTIVATION_DERIVATION_V002.md:85-96` states that, at the
first completed-transfer interval, the zero-charge sector keeps the ready
record unchanged while the nonzero sectors write the charged record:

```text

  |0,r> -> |0,r>;

Q_Sigma=+1:
  |+,r> -> |+,p_Q>;

Q_Sigma=-1:
  |-,r> -> |-,p_Q>.
```

`BID_UNIQUE_CHARGED_CONTROLLED_COUPLING_DERIVATION_V001.md:74-89` sharpens the
same charged-access point:

```text

generator vanishes:

```

`CAUSAL_DIRECT_LIMIT_RECORD_PRINCIPLE_V002.md:10-18` defines a durable public
record as a compatible outgoing sector of a causally sequential exhaustion.
Its durability consists jointly of thresholded source nonreturn and exact
completed-record persistence, and its public content is the recoverable
quasi-local record state and central sequence.

### Determination

This test splits by type.

At the algebra/carrier level, sealed text says record degrees exist as labeled
record factors with ready states before a particular write. That is incompatible
with the claim that record algebraic content is generated solely by source
automorphisms.

At the durable-public-record level, sealed text says a public record is formed
only after the source-controlled/write-and-persist structure succeeds. The
zero charged source leaves the charged record ready and opens no charged
handle.

Therefore Test 3 does not force a crossed product. It forces this typed
statement instead:

```text

durable_public_record_without_required_source_write_derived = false
crossed_product_forced_by_no_record_without_source = false
```
