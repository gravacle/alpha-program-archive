## §1 — QUESTION 1. OVERDETERMINATION, SPECIFIED

Two conditions `Ca`, `Cb` on one quantity `X` constitute **overdetermination** iff **[O1]–[O3]**
all hold. ("Independently motivated" is replaced by these; it does no further work.)

```text
[O1] PREMISE-DAG DISJOINTNESS BELOW A TARGET-BLIND KERNEL.
     Trace each condition's derivation DAG to the adopted premise stack. Shared premises are
     admissible ONLY if target-blind (adopted without reference to X's value — a PROVENANCE
     fact, checked by seal-dating and the [O3] sweep, not by assertion). Neither DAG consumes
     the other's OUTPUT. DELETION TEST: remove Cb and all nodes reachable only from it; Ca's
     derivation survives intact — and conversely.
     NOT INDEPENDENT IF: one derives from the other (Q-58: "T_R IS DERIVED FROM C_R = 1 — ONE
     CHOICE, NOT TWO"); both descend from a common premise that itself fixes X; or agreement is
     enforced by an identity of the formalism — then agreement is a THEOREM: a CHECK, valuable
     as verification, NEVER overdetermination (the corpus's own line: consistency-as-CHECK
     everywhere, consistency-as-CONSTRAINT nowhere).
[O2] THE DISAGREEMENT WITNESS — *** THE DISCRIMINATOR. *** (§2, in full.)
[O3] PROCESS-LEVEL TARGET BLINDNESS. Not a mental state — a program whose authors know the
     measured value cannot certify ignorance, and a criterion demanding it would reject every
     human physics program. What IS decidable: (a) ORDER — conditions, constants and branch
     choices hash-sealed before any evaluation (the E1-rule pattern); (b) THE TARGET-ENTRY
     SWEEP — an EXECUTED, RECORDED sweep showing no measured-coupling value enters construction
     or branch selection on the path (the sealed prohibitions at FBRAP V002:145-153 made
     grep-auditable, per Q-52's pattern); (c) NO POST-HOC TUNING — no form or branch revised
     after partial evaluation.
BOUNDARY CLAUSE (the marker/C_R lesson, §3): n conditions fixing n freedoms through
COMPLEMENTARY coordinates is EXACT DETERMINATION — well-posedness, not evidence. The marker
E_R T_R = pi hbar is orbit-invariant and cannot pick T_R alone; joined with C_R = 1 it yields
one value with ZERO redundancy and NO expressible disagreement witness. A specification that
```
