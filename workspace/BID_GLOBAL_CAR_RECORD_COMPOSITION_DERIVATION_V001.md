# BID Global CAR and Record-Cell Composition Derivation v001

Date: 2026-07-23

## Purpose

This derivation prevents the local source spinor from being copied as a
distinguishable degree of freedom in every record cell. It gives the typed
global composition required before connected source-record dynamics can be
derived.

It does not choose a source state, interaction kernel, physical mass, charged
response, or alpha.

## Global source algebra

On a Cauchy surface `Sigma`, let `K_+` be the completion of compactly
supported charge-`q` Dirac Cauchy data in the positive current inner product

```text
<f,g>_Sigma
  =integral_Sigma bar(f) slash(n) g dSigma.
```

Let `K_-` be its charge-conjugate one-particle space with antiunitary
conjugation `J:K_+->K_-`, and put

```text
K_Sigma=K_+ direct-sum K_-.
```

Use the convention that the inner product is antilinear in its first slot and
that the annihilator `a(f)` is antilinear in `f`. The source algebra is the
CAR algebra generated by `a(f)`:

```text
{a(f),a(g)}=0,
{a(f),a(g)^*}=<f,g>_Sigma I.
```

The fermion-parity automorphism is

```text
vartheta_F(a(f))=-a(f).
```

The charge-conjugate one-particle space is now explicit. A physical
particle/antiparticle interpretation, vacuum, or Fock representation still
requires a state/positive-frequency choice; none is inserted here.

The vector-`U(1)` automorphism is

```text
u_theta=exp(i q theta) on K_+,
u_theta=exp(-i q theta) on K_-,
gamma_theta(a(f))=a(u_theta f).
```

For each causally admissible region `O` on `Sigma`, let `K(O)` be the
subspace of Cauchy data supported in `O`. The local source algebra is
`CAR(K(O))`. For disjoint Cauchy supports the odd generators anticommute and
the even local subalgebras commute. This is the finite-Cauchy-surface local
net used below; causal propagation and the continuum quasilocal completion
remain part of the complete parent.

## Labeled record factors

For each causal record cell `c`, retain the distinguishable record carrier

```text
R_c=span_C{|r_c>,|p_Q,c>,|e_Q,c>}.
```

For a finite labeled cell set `C`, the public record algebra is

```text
R(C)=tensor_(c in C) B(R_c).
```

The total source-record algebra is

```text
A_SR(C)=CAR(K_Sigma,q) graded-tensor R(C).
```

Give every record operator trivial fermion grading. The graded tensor product
with `R(C)` then reduces canonically to the ordinary tensor product, and every
record operator commutes with the source algebra. The source CAR algebra
appears once globally. This does not forbid the legitimate graded factorization
`CAR(K_c direct-sum K_d) ~= CAR(K_c) graded-tensor CAR(K_d)` for orthogonal
local source subspaces; it forbids copying the same source modes independently
into every record factor.

## Quadratic number-preserving local coupling subclass

A number-preserving quadratic local source-record incidence term has the typed
form

```text
B_c
  =sum_(i,j) a(f_(c,i))^* a(f_(c,j))
     tensor b_c(i,j),
```

where:

```text
f_(c,i) are local Dirac test sections;
b_c(i,j) act only on R_c;
the one-particle kernel is self-adjoint;
and its chiral-odd part uses M_(n_c,delta_c).
```

Each `B_c` is even in the source field, vector-`U(1)` neutral, and may be
localized without violating fermion parity.

This formula fixes one physically important lift. Fermion parity alone also
admits higher even powers, derivative/current couplings, gauge-mediated
terms, and multi-record operators. The complete parameter-free action must
classify or generate them. The formula does not fix the test sections, their
normalization, `delta_c`, or every matrix element of `b_c`.

## Disjoint and connected cells

For disjoint cells whose source test spaces are orthogonal on `Sigma`, the
local CAR relations give

```text
[B_c,B_d]=0.
```

If no shared boundary, gauge, or environment term couples them, their
evolution factorizes and the completed-record transition amplitudes multiply.

For overlapping supports or shared fields, the commutator need not vanish.
The connected parent must therefore be generated by one complete action:

```text
B_parent
  =B_Dirac(g,A)
   +sum_c B_c
   +B_boundary/environment(g,A,record).
```

Its overlap terms, ordering, preparation, and connected cumulants are outputs
of that action. They are not inferred from the one-cell incidence matrix and
may not be set to zero merely to preserve a preferred result.

## Consequence for the earlier finite parent

The six-dimensional matrix in
`BID_CHIRAL_SOURCE_RECORD_INCIDENCE_PARENT_V001.md` is a one-source,
one-record-sector representation of a local incidence kernel. In the global
theory it must be recovered as a restriction of the even CAR coupling above.
It is not the tensor factor assigned independently to every causal cell.
