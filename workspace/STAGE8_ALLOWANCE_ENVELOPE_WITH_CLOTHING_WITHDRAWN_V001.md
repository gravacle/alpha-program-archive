## 1. Lead Finding: New Class

Q-80 applies. With the eight clothed adoptions withdrawn, the theory's honest
current output is not a number and not a plain low-dimensional parameter
space. It is a:

```text
MIXED_TYPE_ALLOWANCE_ENVELOPE
```

Definition: a set-valued theory output whose independent shrink directions are
not all numerical coordinates. Its factors may include scalar parameters,
discrete branch choices, operator/action-form classes, algebraic composition
choices, admissibility laws, and categorical/functorial structure. It is
stateable by typed axes, but it has no single numeric dimension unless the
non-scalar factors are themselves specified.

What this class resists:

1. **Low-dimensional parametrized family.** The eight axes include open
   operator/action-form and algebraic/categorical freedoms, not only scalar
   parameters.
2. **Unbounded direction only.** Some factors may be open or unbounded, but
   "unbounded" is not the whole structure; the envelope also contains discrete
   and categorical choices.
3. **Not characterizable.** The eight axes are enumerated and each removes a
   named freedom. What is missing is a numeric measure or complete internal
   parametrization of several axes, not the envelope's typed shape.

This is an object-finding, not a defect finding. The imported "dimension of a
parameter space" framing is too narrow for the present object.
