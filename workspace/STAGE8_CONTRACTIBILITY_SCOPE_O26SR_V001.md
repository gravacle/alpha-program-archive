### 2.2 READING THE ARITHMETIC — WHICH INPUTS THE VANISHING ACTUALLY CONSUMES

`H^1 = 1 - 2 + 1 = 0` is the cycle-rank formula `E - V + C`. Its three inputs are
therefore, and only:

```text
E = 1   the reciprocal edge pair COUNTED AS ONE 1-CELL
V = 2   the node count
C = 1   connectedness
```

**NOTHING ELSE FROM THE RULE ENTERS.** Not "matched carriers." Not "identity cell
matching." Not "one-tier delay." Not the transport `tau`. Not the cells. Those
five clauses appear nowhere in `1 - 2 + 1`. This is the first and sharpest thing
to say about the cause: the dismissal's arithmetic is blind to almost the whole
rule.

**WHAT CLAUSES R AND A DO TOGETHER, EXACTLY.** Under reciprocity, a node's

nodes and single edges). Verified exhaustively by instrument (§6): for every
V from 1 to 8, **every** reciprocal max-degree-<=1 incidence has b_1 = 0, in the
simple category, without exception.

**THIS IS A REAL STRUCTURAL RESULT AND IT IS NOT THE SOURCE'S.** It is stronger
than "it makes a tree": it says every component has AT MOST ONE EDGE. But note
