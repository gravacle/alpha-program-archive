## The loophole, and its closure

```text
AS SEALED, Rule 6's recovery clause read: "UNTIL this lane's own
prediction family recovers a comparable run of landed predictions, judged
from CALIBRATION_LEDGER.md and not by the lane's own assessment."
THE DEFECT: that still routes through this lane. If this lane reads the
ledger and concludes it has recovered, that is SELF-ASSESSMENT WITH EXTRA
STEPS. Pointing at an external document does not make the judgment
external.

AMENDMENT, now binding:
  THE RECOVERY JUDGMENT IS HELD BY THE PRINCIPAL, on the REVIEWER LANE'S
  reading of CALIBRATION_LEDGER.md.
  NO LANE — INCLUDING THIS ONE — MAY DECLARE ITS OWN PREDICTION FAMILY
  RECOVERED.
  UNTIL THAT JUDGMENT IS ISSUED, RULE 6 BINDS: every disagreement with an
  independent lane's prediction is stated side by side, the independent
  prediction is weighted, and the artifact records that it was.
  RULE 6 THEREFORE CANNOT EXPIRE SILENTLY — expiry requires a positive,
  external, recorded act.
```
