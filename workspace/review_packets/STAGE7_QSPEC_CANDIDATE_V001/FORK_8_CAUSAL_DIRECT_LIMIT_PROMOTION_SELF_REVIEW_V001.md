## Findings

### Finding 1: the executable is a dependency audit, not the proof

The promotion script checks sealed upstream results against the seven
predeclared conditions. A passing Boolean cannot create the theorem. The
substantive arguments are the global measure classification, finite-parent
theorem, source-tail spectral theorem, outgoing record-GNS theorem, and the
cofinal-stabilization argument written in the result.

### Finding 2: response equivalence is narrow

Different cofinal physical exhaustions are proved equivalent on completed
local record states and dressed maps after a causal buffer. The result does
not prove that the half-line, tree, lattice, and continuum regulators share
one full spectral measure or one coupling response.

### Finding 3: spectral law and boundary state must not be conflated

The parent fixes `h_0`; physical boundary data fixes `psi`; together they fix
`mu_psi`. Allowing different incoming states is not an adjustable law.
However, no particular `mu_psi` may be selected later to fit a coupling.
Fork-8 promotion authorizes durability only, not a response evaluation.

### Finding 4: the global source limit remains open

The source tail theorem applies to every declared finite-energy excitation,
and the record state has an exact inductive limit. There is still no proof of
a projective limit of complete source-record states or an infinite-future
source Moller unitary. The promoted principle does not contain either claim.

### Finding 5: promotion is branch-scoped

The proof uses a flat source-free asymptotic Dirac generator and the global
classification of flat primitive diamonds. Curved, nonstationary, and
interacting massless-gauge asymptotics remain outside the result.

### Finding 6: the promoted object is not alpha

Fork 8 closes the mechanism by which a finite reversible write becomes a
durable, recoverable outgoing public record. It neither fixes the absolute
charged-response magnitude nor freezes the complete `Q_spec`.
