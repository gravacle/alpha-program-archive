# Causal Direct-Limit Record Hypothesis v001

Date: 2026-07-24

## Status

This is a target-independent **hypothesis under adjudication**. It is not an
adopted principle, a theorem, or a coupling result.

## Sealed motivation

The finite one-cell BID operator writes the first orthogonal endpoint and
then exactly reverses it:

```text
U(tau_R)|r> = |p>,
U(tau_R)|p> = |r>,
U(2 tau_R) = I.
```

A finite closed discrete-spectrum extension is recurrent unless an invariant
pointer sector or exact superselection rule is independently derived. The
finite BID parent therefore supplies a local reversible write, not a durable
public record.

## Hypothesis

The finite BID cell is the local act of writing. A completed durable record
is an outgoing sector of a future-directed causal continuation of cells
governed by the same unit-normalized incidence law.

Mathematically, the proposal requires:

1. a directed family of finite causal complexes `C_n`;
2. isometric embeddings `J_nm : H_n -> H_m` for `n <= m`;
3. compatible local incidence operators, with no new coupling or response
   normalization;
4. an inductive-limit Hilbert space or observable algebra;
5. a self-adjoint limit generator obtained independently of any coupling
   value;
6. public record labels represented by recoverable outgoing or tail sectors;
7. thresholded durability rather than exact finite-size orthogonality.

For a local return amplitude

```text
A_n(t) = <r|exp(-i t B_n)|r>,
```

the cycle-7 durability requirement is carried in the asymptotic form:

```text
for every delta > 0, there exists T_delta < infinity such that
sup_(t >= T_delta) |A_infinity(t)|^2 < delta.
```

Finite approximants may recur. The physical order of limits is to construct
the infinite causal theory first and then test the `(T, delta)` condition.

Local return decay is necessary but not sufficient. The construction must
also show that the written alternative remains recoverable in an outgoing or
tail algebra. Merely losing amplitude from the root does not prove a public
record exists.

A1  causal half-line;

A4  Lorentz-covariant causal-complex continuum;

These are competitors, regulators, or effective descriptions until the

of a numerical response.

## Promotion bar

The hypothesis may be promoted to a Gravacle principle only if:

1. disclosed locality, causal composition, covariance, and public-record
   requirements derive the physical architecture or one response-equivalent
   class;
2. the direct limit exists and its generator is self-adjoint;
3. durability follows with predeclared `(T, delta)` quantifiers;
4. the outgoing record labels are recoverable;
5. no adjustable spectral density, branching rule, cell measure, damping
   rate, or normalization remains;
6. at least one architecture-sensitive consequence is computed that was not
   assumed in the hypothesis;
7. all surviving architectures are reported, with no value-based selector.

Failure of any item leaves the object a hypothesis and leaves the parent
action gate open.

## Prohibitions

No measured coupling, endpoint, mass, or cosmological value may enter this
adjudication. No architecture may be selected because its decay law or later
response resembles a desired value.
