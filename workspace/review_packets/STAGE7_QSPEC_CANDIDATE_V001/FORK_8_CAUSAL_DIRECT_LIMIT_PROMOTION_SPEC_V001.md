## Scope of possible promotion

The only possible promotion is:

```text
ordinary 3+1 flat-asymptotic branch;
Lorentz-covariant causal-cell continuum;
finite-energy L2 source excitations;
completed-record quasi-local algebra;
and physical exhaustions that add future primitive cells without
subdividing an already primitive cell.
```

Promotion in this scope is not a universal curved-spacetime theorem and is
not a global infinite-future source-scattering theorem.

## Seven promotion tests

### P1. Physical architecture or response-equivalent class

The adjudication must verify that the disclosed Lorentzian covariance and
causal composition select the continuum causal-cell class. Half-line, tree,
and spatial-lattice constructions must remain regulators; an effective
environment must remain downstream.

Within the physical class, different cofinal finite exhaustions must give
the same completed-record restrictions and dressed local maps after their
causal buffer. A unique combinatorial triangulation is not required if this
response-equivalence theorem holds.

### P2. Direct limit and self-adjoint generator

The outgoing record algebras must form an inductive system with one
compatible state and GNS representation. Completed-record evolution must
have a self-adjoint generator.

The source tail must come from the same parent and have its stated
self-adjoint Dirac generator. A global infinite-future source Moller unitary
is not silently required or asserted.

### P3. Thresholded durability

The same parent must establish both:

```text
for every admitted finite-energy L2 source root and every delta>0,
there exists finite T_delta such that
sup_(t>=T_delta)|A_source(t)|^2<delta;

and every completed record restriction remains exactly invariant under
later causal cells.
```

Source nonreturn and public-record persistence must remain distinct.

### P4. Recoverable outgoing labels

The completed-record state must live on the quasi-local outgoing algebra,
and a central sequence must separate the public labels. Root-amplitude loss
alone is insufficient.

### P5. No separately adjustable spectral law or architecture knob

The intrinsic cell measure, causal envelope, parent generator, in-state
class, tail generator, and outgoing state must descend from the same sealed
parent chain.

For a specified physical incoming state `psi`, its spectral measure

```text
mu_psi(B)=<psi,P_h0(B)psi>
```

is state-dependent boundary data computed from the fixed generator. This is
not an adjustable spectral-density law. Promotion fails if an independent
`rho(E)`, topology, branching number, damping rate, regulator weight, or
normalization must be chosen after the parent.

### P6. Unassumed architecture-sensitive consequence

At least one consequence not assumed in the original hypothesis must have
been derived. The predeclared candidates are:

```text
the finite counting ray has no nonzero Hilbert-covector direct limit and
instead lifts to a quasi-local state and central sequence;

or the incoming record representation is necessarily a coherent
source-dressed net rather than a bare record-only endomorphism.
```

No numerical agreement may serve as this consequence.

### P7. Complete reporting

Every architecture in the original blind set must retain its classification.
No winner may be chosen by a coupling, endpoint, or decay-value match.
