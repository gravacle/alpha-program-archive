time interval `I` and a common-core vector `psi`, the necessary estimate

```text
||U_n(t,s)Q_n psi - U(t,s)psi||
 <= ||(I-Q_n)U(t,s)psi||
    + integral_s^t ||Q_n h_K(r)(I-Q_n)U(r,s)psi|| dr.
```

strongly continuous continuum evolution are present. The resisting term is


## 5. Schatten/phase argument: sharp-cell obstruction [CLAIMED]

For the massless Dirac polarization, the off-diagonal Shale–Stinespring condition for a sharp
cell multiplier `M_chi` is

```text
P_- M_chi P_+ in S_2,
```

equivalently `[P_-,M_chi] in S_2`. Away from the diagonal's distributional identity term, the
kernel of `P_-` has the local homogeneous size `|x-y|^-3` in three spatial dimensions. Across any
locally regular nontrivial cell boundary, write `u,v >= 0` for the two normal distances,
`w=u+v`, and `z` for tangential separation. The cross-boundary part of the squared Hilbert–Schmidt
norm contains

```text
integral_0^epsilon w dw
  integral_(R^2) dz / (|z|^2+w^2)^3
 = C integral_0^epsilon w^(-3) dw
 = infinity.
```

Thus the sharp causal-cell multiplier in member 11 does not have Hilbert–Schmidt off-diagonal
polarization at a regular boundary. A finite-rank sequence cannot be Cauchy in `S_2` with that
strong sharp-cell limit: an `S_2`-Cauchy sequence would have an `S_2` limit, contradicting the
displayed boundary divergence. Consequently the implementer phase or determinant-line completion
cannot be certified by the requested Schatten route for this sharp multiplier.

A smoothed cell profile could change the Schatten class, but its smoothing profile, thickness,
scale, and convergence rule are absent from the closed record. Supplying them here would be a new
selector/datum, so they are not used as a rescue.
