## 0. THE HEADLINE, BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

The clause is **NOT AMBIGUOUS AT BYTES.** It reads as a **CONCESSION** — but a
concession whose surrendered term is **SUFFICIENCY, NOT CAPABILITY.**

The apparent two-way ambiguity the commission poses is an artifact of a
**SECOND ELISION.** The clause does not end at "two cells." At its own bytes it
ends four words later, and those four words carry the entire dismissal:

```text
Even if adopted, it glues two cells into a contractible object.
```

Restore "into a contractible object" and the polarity question answers itself.
The source is not weighing whether the object joins cells — it states flatly that
it does. It is stating what the join YIELDS, and the yield is the ground of
dismissal. See §2.

## 2. DELIVERABLE ONE — THE CLAUSE'S POLARITY

### 2.1 The clause in its own surrounding sentences, quoted whole

```text
     Status of record: PROPOSED_NOT_ADOPTED (DoR-016/017 RESERVED) — the artifact

     network, Adj_2 = [[0,1],[1,0]], "no self-edge", reciprocal, one-tier delayed.

```

The source's own casing is lowercase throughout the final clause. The audit's
rendering "IT GLUES TWO CELLS" carries the audit's own declared emphasis
("Emphasis added by this audit; the words are the source's") — that is accurate,
and no capitalisation in the source is being relied on here.

### 2.2 VERDICT: CONCESSION — BUT THE CONCEDED TERM IS SUFFICIENCY

The sentence is compound. It asserts one thing and denies another, and the two
must not be collapsed:

```text
CONSTITUENT              FORCE        WHAT IT DOES
"Even if adopted,"       CONCESSIVE   Grants the adoption gate ARGUENDO. It does
                                      not deny the gluing and does not assert the
                                      adoption. It neutralises adoption as the
                                      thing that would decide the matter.
"it glues two cells"     ASSERTED     The joining predicate, stated flat, unhedged,
                                      in the indicative. NOT denied. NOT conditional.
"into a contractible     RESULT       THE DISMISSAL. The entire insufficiency lives
 object."                COMPLEMENT   here, in the TYPE OF THE YIELD.
```

So, against the commission's two readings:

```text
READING (i)  CAPABILITY — "the object does join cells."
             ITS FACTUAL CORE IS CORRECT AND IS AFFIRMED AT BYTES. The source
             states the object glues cells. This is not hedged anywhere in the span.
             WHERE (i) FAILS: it reads "adoption is what stands between the corpus
             and having a joiner." The source says the opposite — "EVEN IF adopted"
             concedes adoption precisely in order to show adoption is NOT what
             stands in the way. Adoption is granted and the object still falls short.

READING (ii) CONCESSION — "the source is dismissing it as insufficient."
             ITS FORCE IS CORRECT. The clause IS a dismissal.
             WHERE (ii) IS MIS-GROUNDED: the commission glosses the ground as
             "it joins only two." That is not the source's ground. The source's
             ground is CONTRACTIBILITY — H^1 = 0. The number two enters only
             INSTRUMENTALLY, as the arithmetic that forces the tree.
```

**THE SETTLING SENTENCE.** The clause immediately preceding, in the same row:

```text
"As a graph: 2 vertices, one reciprocal edge pair = a tree; H^1 = 1 - 2 + 1 = 0."
```

This is the sentence that decides the polarity, and it decides it as CONCESSION.
The source computes the yield's first cohomology, gets zero, and only THEN writes
"Even if adopted." The concessive is the consequence of the H^1 line, not a
judgement about how many cells were joined. Two cells joined by one reciprocal
edge is a tree; a tree is contractible; a contractible yield is not what the sweep
is hunting.

**CORROBORATING SENTENCE — the hunt's own stated quarry.** The same artifact's
sweep answer, immediately after row G-10:

```text
"a sealed multi-cell glued record complex EXISTS — two of them, K_square and K_L,
 both inside V011, both loop-carrying — but both are sealed at the AUDIT/PREDICTION
 stratum."
```

The hunt's criterion of interest is **loop-carrying** (H^1 > 0). G-9's yield is
H^1 = 0. It is dismissed for failing the criterion the whole sweep is organised
around, not for a shortfall in cell count.

### 2.3 ON AMBIGUITY — DECLARED, NOT PREFERRED

The commission instructed: if the source is ambiguous at bytes, display both
readings and do not resolve by preference. **The source is NOT ambiguous at bytes,**
and this is a byte finding, not a preference:

```text

THE CLAUSE AT BYTES   "Even if adopted, it glues two cells into a
                       contractible object."                         -> NOT AMBIGUOUS
```

The ambiguity is manufactured entirely by the truncation. The four words
"into a contractible object" are the predicate that carries the dismissal, and
with them present no capability-reading of the sentence's FORCE survives — while
the capability-reading of its PREDICATE is simultaneously confirmed.

**TWO ELISIONS IN SERIES, BOTH NOW RESTORED.** This matters for the record:

```text
ELISION 2  The clause as carried into THIS commission's framing ends at
```

The audit itself did NOT commit elision 2: it quoted the clause whole, including

### 3.2 Does it take cells and yield a joined structure? — YES, AND THAT IS THE POINT

```text

DOES IT YIELD A JOINED STRUCTURE? YES. The source says so in the indicative:
                                 "it glues two cells."
```

This is the row's whole significance, and it is why the audit called its omission

recorded the output side as its **combinatorial data** (`Adj_2 = [[0,1],[1,0]]`)
and not as **what the data is a gluing of.** An adjacency matrix and a glued pair
of cells are the same object under two descriptions; only the second description
answers the question "does anything in the corpus join cells."

### 3.3 IS THE COUNT "TWO" A LIMITATION OF THE OBJECT, OR OF THE DISPLAY?

**OF THE DISPLAY — and the source declares this in its own voice.** This is the
sharpest finding in the typing, and neither the census nor the audit reaches it,
because both stop at the hunt and neither descends to NET V001's own bytes.


So "two" is **an instantiation scope, not a structural ceiling.** The proposal

**BUT THE GENERALISATION IS NOT FREE, AND MUST NOT BE READ AS AVAILABLE.**
Three things hold simultaneously, and dropping any one of them misstates the
object:

```text

    subjunctive — "WOULD require." It is a statement of what a general family
    would need, not a display of one.
(b) THE SELECTOR IS EXPLICITLY NOT MADE.  ":347 — so NO PERMUTATION OR
    INTERTWINER IS SELECTED." The very thing NS-8 says a general family requires

(c) "LARGER GRAPH" IS A DECLARED LIVE ALTERNATIVE, NOT A CLOSED ONE.

    "any positive integer delay; LARGER GRAPH; self-edge."
    The larger graph sits in the unselected column. It is neither foreclosed nor
    taken.
```

**TYPING CONSEQUENCE.** The count is display-limited, so the object is not
intrinsically a two-cell joiner; but the display is all there is, so the corpus
holds no instantiated joiner of more than two. Both halves are true and the
artifact records both. Nothing here is a recommendation to instantiate more.
