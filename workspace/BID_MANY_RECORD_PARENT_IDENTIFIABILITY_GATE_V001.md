strong symmetric-monoidal functor into `(Hilb,tensor)` with product
preparations and factorized continuous evolution, then

```text

U_(K_1 disjoint-union K_2)(t) = U_(K_1)(t) tensor U_(K_2)(t).
```

Differentiating at `t=0` gives the unique finite-dimensional generator


Consequently amplitudes multiply and `-log|A|` adds on genuinely disjoint
systems. This is a conditional theorem. Symmetric monoidality by itself does
not select the tensor product as the target monoidal operation; that target
and the strength of the functor must be derived or disclosed. The theorem
does not select the connected parent.

```text

vacuum and one-record sectors.
```

An explicit two-mode hard-core counterfamily is


```text

has the same vacuum restriction;
```

Thus a one-record incidence matrix, disjoint additivity, locality, exchange
symmetry, and target independence do not force the connected many-record
operator. Choosing `lambda=0` because it is minimal would be a selection, not
a derivation.

A connected BID parent requires all four items below before evaluation:

1. **Record statistics.** Derive whether primitive durable records obey
   unrestricted occupancy, antisymmetric occupancy, or one-record-per-cell

2. **Active-handle control.** Derive the source/access projectors that select
   the handle-conditioned operator, or derive the complete composite-handle
   first-opening operator and interval.
3. **Global carrier and gluing.** Construct the many-record carrier functor
   from `OpenRec_2` or `DecRec_2`, including how shared boundary cells are
   identified.
4. **Connected generator.** Derive the complete local generator from the
   same incidence/action principle. Prove whether overlap terms such as
   `lambda |11><11|` are generated, forbidden, or fixed.
5. **Ordering and preparation.** Derive whether overlapping local terms enter
   a continuous sum generator, time-ordered exponential, sequential update,
   or circuit, and derive the connected preparation rather than selecting a
   convenient product or clustering state.
6. **Thermodynamic control.** Prove a uniform linked-cluster or equivalent
   zero-free limit at the independently derived record interval. A
   small-time theorem does not pass unless its certified domain contains
   that interval.
