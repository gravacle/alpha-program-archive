## §1 — WHY: THE APPOSITIVE IS DEFINITIONAL

The ground requires that *"is itself the obstruction"* carry semantic weight **independent** of the
appositive, so that R-17's retyping can detach the bullet from it. It does not.

### 1.1 The predecessor spells it out with "i.e."

`STAGE8_T7_E1_SUCCESSOR_PROGRAM_SPEC_V001.md:870`, the immediate ancestor of the identical clause:

> "localizer M(t) is itself the obstruction — **i.e.** a certified-divergent E1 against
> the sharp localizer —"

**V002 kept the em-dash and dropped the "i.e."** A dropped abbreviation is not a change of
grammatical function. The governing standard uses the same construction in parentheses
(`38e15177…:34`).

### 1.2 The four-bullet test — the decisive one, run independently twice

If *"obstruction"* were broader than the appositive, at least one of the four trigger bullets would
reach that extra content. **None does.**

```text
BULLET 1  C-L3 nonzero            -> INSTANCE. Spec :1151 "E1 is UPGRADED to CERTIFIED
                                     DIVERGENT AT THE SEALED DEFINITION OF M(t)".
BULLET 2  B-L2* certified FAILURE -> INSTANCE. Its own qualifier — CERTIFIED rather than merely
                                     uncertified — IS the certified-divergent distinction.
BULLET 3  no X_*(eps) for R-L0    -> INSTANCE, AND THE SHARPEST CONTROL IN THE LIST. A plain
          BECAUSE of the sharp       R-L0 failure is NOT a Q2 trigger — :1842 routes it to
          localizer                  E1S_BLOCKED. What converts it is exactly the two added
                                     conditions: CERTIFIED, and BECAUSE OF the sharp localizer.
                                     *** THE DISCRIMINATOR BETWEEN BLOCKED AND Q2-STOP IS THE
                                     CERTIFIED-DIVERGENT-E1 FACT ITSELF, NOT A RICHER NOTION
                                     OF OBSTRUCTION. ***
BULLET 4  numerator unsatisfiable -> INSTANCE. D6' is titled "Sharp localizers (frozen)", so
          at the sealed D6'          "at the sealed D6' definitions" IS "against the sharp
                                     localizer".
```

**Four routes to one state, and that state is the appositive's.** The list is open ("not limited
to") because the *routes* are non-exhaustive — which is what an open list under a **definitional**
condition looks like.

