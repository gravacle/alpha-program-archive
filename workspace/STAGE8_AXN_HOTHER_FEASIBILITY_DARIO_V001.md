## 4. The complete positive solution class, and the obstruction [PROVABLE]

such family satisfies `(I)` and `(II)`.*

*Proof.* (⟸) For `k in Z^N`, `lambda_N^(k) = mu^(k_1)`; both `(I)` and `(II)` reduce to
`mu^(m_1) = mu^(m_1)`.

(⟹) Take `M = N+1` and `m = 0` in `(II)`, and use `(I)` at `m = 0` for the total masses:

```text
integral r_(N+1) d lambda_(N+1)  =  lambda_N(Q_N)  =  lambda_(N+1)(Q_(N+1))  =  integral 1 d lambda_(N+1),
```

so `integral (1 - r_(N+1)) d lambda_(N+1) = 0`. Take real parts. For `r` on the unit circle

measure integrates to zero only if it vanishes almost everywhere, so `r_(N+1) = 1`

almost surely the identity; coordinate 1 is never an appended coordinate and receives no constraint.
