## Fixed distinctions

Three objects must not be conflated:

1. **Comparison group:** the group represented by additive action
   comparisons.
2. **Endpoint carrier:** the Hilbert space of first public alternatives.
3. **Graded chain carrier:** the direct sum of vertex and transition-cell
   spaces on which an incidence operator acts.

A finite-dimensional endpoint carrier can carry a continuous `U(1)`
representation. Its finite dimension does not make the comparison group
finite.

## Explicit branch premise for the comparison group

The ordinary charged comparison action is assumed to be the strongly
continuous, faithful unitary representation of

```text
G_S = R / (2 pi hbar Z) ~= U(1)
```

induced by

```text
rho([Delta S]) = exp(i Delta S / hbar).
```

This is an explicit ordinary-branch premise. It is not derived from an
acyclic first-opening graph. Under this premise, `Z2 x Z2` and `Z4` may be
finite readout quotients or sampled coarse grainings, but they are not exact
faithful replacements for `G_S`.

## First-order record germ

The inherited ordinary branch supplies:

```text
r = 3 independent primitive public action handles;
one unresolved pre-record alternative;
one first variation generated by each independent handle;
no additional independent first variation without an additional handle;
and no composite variation at first order.
```

The last two statements are the **First-Order Record Minimality
Hypothesis**. They are new, target-aware physical content. They do not count
as derived consequences.

The carrier theorem must enumerate connected rooted oriented first-order
complexes realizing this germ. If it proves that every minimal realization
is isomorphic to the rooted star `K_(1,r)`, then:

```text
dim C_0 = r + 1;
dim C_1 = r;
dim(C_0 direct-sum C_1) = 2r + 1.
```

For `r = 3`, the resulting `4 + 3 = 7` dimensions are historically
target-aware. They are a reproduction result, not a new BID prediction and
not evidence for alpha.

## Typed local-system chain data

For a candidate finite rooted oriented cell complex `K`, let `L_rho` be the
rank-one Hermitian local system carrying `rho`. Define:

```text
H_K = C_0(K; L_rho) direct-sum C_1(K; L_rho)
Gamma_K = diag(+I_C0, -I_C1).
```

For an oriented edge `e` with source `s(e)`, target `t(e)`, and transported
action element `g_e`, the full weighted incidence family is

```text
partial_(rho,lambda) e
  = lambda_e [v_(t(e)) - rho(g_e) v_(s(e))],

partial_(rho,lambda):
  C_1(K; L_rho) -> C_0(K; L_rho),

lambda_e > 0.
```

The vertex and edge spaces initially carry arbitrary positive diagonal
local measures. The adjoint is always taken with respect to those declared
measures. No orthonormal counting measure is assumed as a result.

The corresponding general odd self-adjoint first-incidence operator is

```text
B_(rho,lambda)
  = [[0, partial_(rho,lambda)],
     [partial_(rho,lambda)^dagger, 0]].
```

The action character fixes `rho(g_e)`, hence phase. It does not fix
`lambda_e`, the cell measures, or an absolute dimensionful scale.

## BID hypothesis to be tested

BID proposes that primitive record dynamics is one local natural operator,
not a sum of independently normalized sector actions.

The exact mathematical hypothesis is:

1. **First incidence:** the primitive operator connects only incident
   `0`- and `1`-cells and anticommutes with `Gamma_K`.
2. **Self-adjointness:** reversal of an oriented primitive transition is the
   Hilbert adjoint.
3. **Character covariance:** local-system rephasing conjugates the operator
   unitarily and changes no spectrum or public comparison.
4. **Primitive naturality:** every embedding of the undecorated one-edge
   record germ into any admissible `K` intertwines the same dimensionless
   one-edge operator. A handle label may change transported character data;
   it may not change primitive transition magnitude.
5. **One-record normalization:** on the universal one-edge germ, in
   dimensionless record-counting units,

   ```text
   norm(partial e)^2 = 2.
   ```

   This fixes only the common dimensionless normalization. The physical
   dimensionful conversion remains downstream of the independently derived
   causal record interval.
6. **Single-operator completeness:** every primitive microscopic term must
   be a component of this natural first-incidence operator. Terms requiring
   a composed loop, curvature, or a closed `2`-cell are generated terms, not
   independent primitive coefficients.

Items 4-6 are the new physical BID content. They are stated before any BID
calculation. The theorem work must determine whether they are mutually
consistent and whether they really yield uniqueness.

## Exact pass criteria

### Gate 1: comparison-group classification

Given the explicit strong-continuity and faithfulness premise, prove that
the exact comparison group is `U(1)` and classify `Z2 x Z2` and `Z4` only as
nonfaithful finite quotients or samplings. Do not infer this from a tree
spectrum.

### Gate 2: first-order carrier classification

Enumerate the minimal rooted complexes representing the first-order record
germ. Pass requires one isomorphism class. More than one class is a failure.

### Gate 3: operator uniqueness

Enumerate the complete relative-weight class. Before one-record
normalization, the quotient must contain exactly one positive ray. After
normalization, it must contain exactly one point. Any inequivalent discrete
alternative or continuous family is a failure.

### Gate 4: relative-rescaling forbidding lemma

The fixed rejection property is primitive naturality under every embedding
of the universal one-edge record germ. The proof must show that each
non-common rescaling fails that intertwining relation. No alternative
property may be selected after computation.

### Gate 5: primitive-addition forbidding lemma

Exhaust the enlarged primitive-action competitor class. The proof must show
that every surviving `F^2`, Pauli/clover, mixed, or diagonal term is either:

```text
generated only after composition from the unique operator;
unitarily or gauge equivalent to it;
or inconsistent with first incidence, grading, naturality, and
single-operator completeness.
```

The theorem must not claim that effective `F^2` or Pauli terms vanish.
Those terms must be allowed to arise from `B^2`, a determinant, a spectral
action, or coarse graining.

### Gate 6: frozen-parent reproduction or correction

Only after Gates 1-5 are sealed, compare the derived operator with the
frozen parent. Agreement supplies provenance. Disagreement corrects the
parent. No coefficient may be changed after comparison.

## Primitive versus effective statement

The strong BID proposal is:

```text
no independently adjustable primitive F^2 coefficient;
no independently adjustable primitive Pauli/clover coefficient;
no independently adjustable primitive handle normalization.
```

It is not:

```text
no F^2 term;
no Pauli term;

```

The proposal remains unproved until Gates 3-5 pass.

## Failure and stop rules

BID remains open or fails if:

```text
the carrier classification has multiple inequivalent minima;
the relative-weight quotient has more than one ray;
the proof assumes counting measure or unit weights without deriving them
from the frozen BID hypotheses;
an independent primitive competitor survives;
finite comparison models are rejected by a tree spectrum;
the parent is consulted before the derived result is sealed;
the precommitted composition prediction is changed after calculation;
or alpha is evaluated before all gates and an external holdout protocol
are sealed.
```

No algebraic residual may repair a failed BID gate.

```text

the complete durable environment;
the full matter spectrum;
the finite electromagnetic response;

```
