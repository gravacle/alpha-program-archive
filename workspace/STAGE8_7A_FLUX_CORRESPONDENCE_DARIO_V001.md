because the declared branch is its own counterexample.**

**`Q_Sigma` is an ELECTRIC charge. `deg L` is a MAGNETIC flux. They are different integrals, of
different-degree integrands, over different-dimensional surfaces.**

```text

                                                                  HYPERSURFACE (3-dim)
the tower's q:   deg L   = (1/2 pi) integral_(S^2) F             a 2-FORM over a
                                                                  2-SPHERE (2-dim)
```

Converting the first into the second requires a field relation. Maxwell structure supplies the
opposite: `d*F = j` links the current to `integral *F` — the **electric** flux — while `dF = 0`
makes `integral F` a **topological invariant independent of `j`**. **Electric charge does not
determine magnetic flux.** (Dirac quantisation constrains their *product*, never either alone.)

**And the branch PRIN declares exhibits the failure generically.** An ordinary charged
durable source has `Q_Sigma = 1` and `deg L = 0`. So a correspondence built to carry
`spec(Q_Sigma) = {0,1}` onto `deg L` would be **falsified by the very sector it is built on** —
its own falsifier (§1.7 (a)) is satisfiable by construction.

**Therefore the exact flux-transport consequence, derived rather than asserted: `V003`'s
zero-or-one sector forces NOTHING about `deg L` or `|q|`.** `LEDGER = unchanged` — five free
classes, zero sealed constraints.

**Two corrections to my own 765, both of which Q-672 states and I confirm from bytes.** The V001
gate is not merely unsealed — it is **REJECTED of record**
(`REJECTED_GLOBAL_PHASE_AS_PHYSICAL_RELATIVE_PHASE`, drafting lane `NOT_OF_RECORD`); my 765
under-described its status. And I read the separation require as **blocking**, when its scope is

**And the conflation risk is sharper than 757's, because it lives inside one object.**
