## Ratified Conventions

C1. Lane findings do not receive Part 2 status-register rows merely because
they are findings. Part 2 is reserved for ruled items.

C2. A named obstruction at a file and line is a sealable result class.

C3. When a sealed clause asserts that a status-register row exists and the row
does not exist, the construction lane writes the missing row rather than
editing the sealed clause, provided the row faithfully indexes an existing
authority and manufactures no ruling.

C4. A declined or contaminated referral is provenance and belongs on the face of
the relevant result when it materially affects independence, timing, or usable
authority.

C5. Carrier-indexed numbers may be used refutationally while remaining
F'-5-inadmissible as constants in certified bounds.

## C3 Guard

C3 is ratified with its stop-and-report guard intact. It may not be used to
rescue a sealed clause that no authority supports. If no authority supports the
row, the correct action is to stop and report, not to invent one.
