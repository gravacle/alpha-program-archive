## Principle

### Fundamental record degrees

Durable records are generated by physical boundary degrees of freedom, not by
an external classical readout postulate. On every admissible primitive causal
record cell, the record carrier is a local physical degree of freedom whose
one-handle kinematics reduce to the already derived primitive projective
carrier.

### One complete specification

complete quantum specification

```text

```

`Q_spec` includes fields, action, state, contour, domain, boundaries,
gauge/ghost structure, edge modes, measure, regulator, record operator,
closure map, branch enumeration, and matching rule. No sector may be assigned
an independent normalization after the specification is sealed.

### Covariance and locality

The ordinary public branch is `3+1` dimensional, Lorentzian,
diffeomorphism-covariant, CPT-compatible, and local at the primitive causal
cell before coarse graining. Compact phase comparison is normalized by the
primitive additive-action character. These are branch inputs; spacetime
dimension and Lorentz/CPT structure are not claimed as new outputs here.

### Unique record cell

The allow/require boundary condition is strengthened as a new microscopic
postulate:

```text
when a primitive durable record forms, the boundary law selects one physical
Lorentz-covariant interval, support region, and closure configuration over
which its action is accumulated.
```

This clause must later be realized by an explicit causal-cell theorem. It
cannot be satisfied by choosing a cell shape, duration, cutoff, or boundary
condition after a response has been calculated.

### Parameter-free microscopic action

After units are fixed by the disclosed dimensionful constants and the derived
record cell, the microscopic specification contains no adjustable
dimensionless coupling, multiplicity, kernel coefficient, finite subtraction,
or response normalization. Discrete data must be derived from the record
carrier and causal-cell construction rather than selected by target
comparison.

### Auxiliary comparison connection

At the microscopic level, the compact charged connection `a` is introduced
only to compare local record/source phases covariantly. It is not assigned an
independent bare parity-even curvature stiffness:

```text

```
