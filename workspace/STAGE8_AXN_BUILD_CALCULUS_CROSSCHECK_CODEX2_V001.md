#### G1-b — the quantifier was widened

The packet's exact grammar is instance-specific:

```text

direct squaring of the parent agrees with the declared decomposition;
independent coefficient used = false.
```

It does not say:

```text

all effective descendants are exactly the components of D^2;
and an extra same-parent descendant is universally forbidden.
```

The distinction is load-bearing. “All square-generated descendants” names
the descendants that are square-generated; it does not prove the converse
“all descendants are square-generated.” CIS says effective record, source,
gauge, gravitational, or environmental interactions generated from the one
complete parent are permitted, must inherit covariance, and must be tested.
CIS does not reduce that full class to `D^2`.

Likewise, `independent_descendant_coefficient_used = false` is a report about
the sealed regression. It is not a universal opcode or theorem forbidding a
descendant whose coefficient is derived from another sector of the complete
parent. Turning the status into the universal “only way an extra descendant
could enter” is exactly the S12 error: a status flag substituted for the
object/theorem that would discharge it.

#### G1-c — exact decomposition countermodel

Even after granting a squareable first-order operator `D`, the rule defines
the descendant **set** through an unspecified “declared decomposition.” Let
the packet-style decomposition be

```text

```

The same operator has the exact alternative declaration

```text

```

Both sums equal `D^2` identically; neither changes `D`. Their descendant sets,
component norms, and the number of objects submitted to F13 differ. Nothing
in G1 supplies canonical projectors, a direct-sum uniqueness theorem, an
irreducibility rule, or a ban on refinement of one declared component. The
factor `1/2` is not offered as physics; it is an exact witness that declaration
is a free coordinate unless a decomposition law fixes it.

An equivalent basis rotation inside any multidimensional descendant subspace
gives the same objection without changing cardinality. Squaring is total on a
properly typed operator; **reading off a unique descendant census is not**.

`G1 = CONFIRMED only for the sealed finite parent instance; REFUTED as the
universal, decomposition-free candidate rule.`
