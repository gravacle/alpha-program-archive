## Part 4 — The object-vs-bound tripwire (binding on any exclusion of the sharp localizer)

```text
A DIVERGENT MAJORANT IS NOT A DIVERGENT THEORY. E1 certifying divergent
against the sharp localizer establishes that a BOUND ARCHITECTURE fails
there — NOT that the completed amplitude or its Hessian is ill-defined
there.

Any artifact excluding the sharp localizer MUST state explicitly which of
these it establishes:
  (a) the DEFINED QUANTITY ITSELF (the completed amplitude / its a = 0
      Hessian) is divergent or ill-defined under the sharp localizer — a
      require-grade exclusion; or
  (b) only that every bound of a STATED CLASS fails — require-grade ONLY
      IF the finiteness demanded is itself a sealed principle rather than
      a convenience of the proof; or
  (c) only that the CURRENT architecture's bound fails — which excludes
      NOTHING and licenses only further IR work.

Excluding the sharp localizer BECAUSE IT RESISTS PROOF, without (a) or a
principled (b), is SELECTING FOR PROVABILITY — route-shopping at the
definition level, the failure class one level deeper than any yet caught.
The tripwire exists because this is exactly where it would appear.
```
