## J2. THE DETERMINATION — **OVERLAPPING**

### J2.1 Why not IDENTICAL — both direction tests fail on sealed types

**Direction 1 — would supplying (A) close (B)?** No. `(JD-3)` is an
incidence/evaluation rule producing **cochain coefficients** from a symbol on a
k-cell. It produces no spacetime length, no `β`, and no dimensional
normalization against Lorentzian causal-diamond geometry. Nor does an evaluation
rule supply **boundedness**: (B)'s tested arm failed because no bounded extension
exists from the trace-class domain, and an evaluation rule is not a boundedness
theorem.

**Direction 2 — would supplying (B) close (A)?** No. `β` is a **dimensionless
scalar conversion factor** for lengths, `R = βcΔτ`. It has the wrong domain to
eat an **order-two Ward symbol class with a cotangent variable**, and it produces
no cochain coefficients. It also discharges none of `(A)`'s further obligations —
presentation independence, contact-ideal annihilation, orientation covariance.

**Domains differ; codomains differ; neither supply satisfies the other's
consumer.** A single object of record cannot have two disjoint domains.

I also record the honest counterweight: the type asymmetry I first reached for is
**not** available as a separator. (A) is `TYPE-U`; (B)'s `TYPE-R` flags are
arm-scoped. Both are, at object level, **open demands**. The separation is carried
by the domains and codomains, not by the type words.

### J2.2 Why not DISTINCT — the shared core is sealed, and it is sharp

Three independent sealed facts make the overlap real rather than rhetorical:

1. **Same structural signature, stated in near-identical words by different lanes
