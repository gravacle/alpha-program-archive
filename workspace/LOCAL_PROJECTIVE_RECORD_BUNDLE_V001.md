## Local ray field

causal record cell. Its physical state is a ray in the primitive complex
two-dimensional carrier. On a local patch `U_i`, choose a normalized lift

```text

```

```text

g_ij = exp(i theta_ij) in U(1).
```

On triple overlaps, equality of the represented ray requires

```text

```

bundle, equivalently a principal `U(1)` comparison bundle. This `U(1)` is a
local representative redundancy of the adopted projective record field. It
is not obtained by mistaking a passive basis change for a new physical force.

## Comparison connection

The sealed principle requires covariant comparison of local record/source
phases. Ordinary derivatives of different local lifts do not patch:

```text
d z_j = g_ij (d z_i + i d theta_ij z_i).
```

Introduce local one-forms `a_i` and

```text
D_i = d - i a_i.
```

The derivatives patch covariantly,

```text
D_j z_j = g_ij D_i z_i,
```

if and only if

```text
a_j = a_i + d theta_ij.
```

Thus a connection on the projective record bundle is required by the adopted
local covariant-comparison clause. Its curvature

```text

```

is globally defined because `d^2 theta_ij = 0`.
