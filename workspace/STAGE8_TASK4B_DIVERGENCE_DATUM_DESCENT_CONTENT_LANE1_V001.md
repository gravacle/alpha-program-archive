## 3. I3 - datum reach theorem

### 3.1 Orbit construction

Let `delta_G` be the distinguished datum at realization `G`, and let
`O(delta)` be its admitted groupoid orbit.  Let `psi_G` be any admissible
action member satisfying the independently required action covariance and
the stabilizer condition

```text
h_Act psi_G=psi_G whenever h_Div delta_G=delta_G.  (I3-1)
```

Define on the orbit

```text
Gen_(G')(alpha_Div delta_G):=alpha_Act psi_G.      (I3-2)
```

If two arrows `alpha,beta:G->G'` send `delta_G` to the same datum, then
`beta^(-1)alpha` stabilizes `delta_G`; `(I3-1)` makes `(I3-2)` well-defined.
Equation `(I3-2)` automatically satisfies R1-COV.

Thus an abstract distinguished datum plus an unspecified groupoid action can
be mapped equivariantly to every stabilizer-compatible action member.  It
does not choose among them.  Unspecified `Depth` and `Accum` interfaces add
no selection: without formulas, they can be co-declared for any compatible
stage family.

This proves:

```text
ABSTRACT_DATUM_PLUS_R1_COV_SELECTS_PROFILE = false | TYPE-R
```

The counterexample is the entire orbit construction `(I3-2)`, not one
chosen profile.
