## 1. WHAT THE SCHEME MUST BE, AND ITS SIX COMPONENTS

A variation scheme supporting pullback is a map `δ : X → Y` together with enough structure to push
constraints backwards. Six components are required; the record forces three and stops at two, with
the sixth blocked downstream.

```text

SC2  X, the domain class
SC3  X's support typing
SC4  X's regularity / function space
SC5  δ itself — the variation, with its boundary prescription
SC6  δ*, the pullback of image constraints to domain constraints
```
