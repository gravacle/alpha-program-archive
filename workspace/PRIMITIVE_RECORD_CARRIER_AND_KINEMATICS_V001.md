## Primitive carrier

The pre-alpha single-handle theorem assumes:

```text
two declared durable endpoint alternatives;
one recoverable scalar relative-action marker after the physical null quotient;
faithful response to that marker;
bounded durable capacity;
continuous reversible pre-durable variation;
boundary completeness/no surplus in the declared context.
```

Under those hypotheses, zero carried dimensions cannot represent the non-null
action marker, and a bounded one-dimensional interval has no nontrivial
connected reversible flow. A primitive faithful carried block is therefore a
real two-plane. Its normalized orientation generator obeys

```text
J^2 = -I.
```

Together with the endpoint population coordinate, the capacity body is the
three-dimensional Bloch ball. Its real order-unit space is
`Herm_2(C)`, and the primitive observable algebra is `M_2(C)`.

This is a branch-scoped carrier result. It does not prove that a complete
multi-handle record cell, its environment, or the `g,A,psi` carrier contains
only two states.

## Primitive additive-action representation

Let `Delta S` be the one recoverable scalar action marker. A continuous
reversible representation of additive action differences on the recovered
orientation plane satisfies

```text
U(Delta S_1 + Delta S_2)
  = U(Delta S_1) U(Delta S_2).
```

Every connected one-parameter representation on that plane has the form

```text
U(Delta S)
  = exp[J k Delta S/hbar].
```

Compact return requires integer winding `k` after the action-phase coordinate
has period `2 pi`. The primitive faithful character is `k=1`; `|k|>1`
represents repeated winding and `k=0` is unfaithful. Orientation reversal
relates `k=1` and `k=-1`.

Thus the primitive comparison transfer is

```text
U_R(Delta S) = exp[J Delta S/hbar].
```

In a complex comparison basis this is equivalent, up to a common phase, to

```text
diag(1, exp[i Delta S/hbar]).
```

For the equal comparison state the overlap is

```text
zeta(Delta S)
  = exp[i Delta S/(2 hbar)]
    cos[Delta S/(2 hbar)],
```

with first orthogonality at `Delta S/hbar=pi`.

## Canonical projective kinematic action

phase, the primitive level-one projective kinematics may be written

```text

  = hbar integral d tau
      [i z^dagger D_tau z
```

The pullback connection in `D_tau` carries the primitive action holonomy above.
This fixes the symplectic/phase normalization of the comparator. It does not
specify a new independent Hamiltonian, record support, mode density, state
preparation, durability amplifier, or measure.
