## 2. THE DETERMINATION

**E-Q1 is INDEPENDENT of R-L2b, and deferring it would defeat the purpose it
was written for.**

```text
THE RELAYED ASSESSMENT IS CORRECT ABOUT THE SUBSTANCE AND DOES NOT IMPLY
DOWNSTREAMNESS. The independent lane's reading — 4-volume weight natural by
measure additivity, response pullback naturality open and collapsing onto
R-L2b — is a claim about whether the relief is WARRANTED ON THE MERITS. It
is not a claim about whether the relief is DECIDABLE. Those come apart here,
and the clause makes them come apart on purpose.
IF E-Q1 WERE DEFERRED UNTIL R-L2b RESOLVED, the scoping question would be
settled by whatever the analysis happened to produce. That is precisely
"answered by execution", which the clause's last line forbids IN THOSE WORDS.
```

**And the sharper operational fact, which reframes the whole item:**

```text
E-Q1 BLOCKS BY BEING UNRULED, NOT BY ITS ANSWER.
It is "MANDATORY BEFORE EXECUTION". Both a GRANT and a DECLINE discharge that
precondition — a decline discharges it by making R-L0 fail honestly with its
named witness, which the spec pre-registers as an acceptable outcome.
=> ANY RULING UNBLOCKS E1's E-Q1 PRECONDITION. DEFERRAL IS THE ONLY OPTION
   THAT KEEPS E1 FROZEN, and it is the one the clause was written to prevent.
```

## 4. THE OPTIONS, WITH COSTS

```text
OPTION 1 — DECLINE THE RELIEF.
  The cell 4-volume is not an admissible constant. R-L0 must close on its
  own grounds or fail.
  COST: if BOTH R-L0 grounds and R-L0b fail, R-L0 FAILS with its named
    witness. That is a real, pre-registered negative outcome, and it
    propagates: R-L0 is F'-5's enforcement mechanism, so its failure is not
    cosmetic.
  BUYS: F'-5 wholly untouched; no laundering channel; the honest outcome is
    pre-registered so it cannot be re-litigated after the fact.

OPTION 2 — GRANT THE RELIEF, UNSCOPED.
  The cell 4-volume is admissible as a constant generally.
  COST: HIGHEST, AND NOT RECOMMENDED. It guts F'-5's enforcement mechanism —
    once one cellulation geometric datum is admissible unscoped, the
    sup-over-admitted-cells requirement no longer bites, and any later
    geometric datum has a precedent to cite. In substance this is weakening
    F'-5, which is the principal's alone but is a heavier act than the slot
    is asking for.
  BUYS: R-L0 becomes satisfiable cheaply. That cheapness is the problem.

OPTION 3 — GRANT, SCOPED BY THE EXISTING PRECEDENT.   [RECOMMENDED]
  Admit the cell 4-volume ONLY inside explicitly scoped statements, exactly
  as A-L3/A-L4's cubic shell counts are already admitted, with a named
  witness marking the scope boundary.
  COST: every verdict relying on it must say "ON THE PINNED SKELETON" and
    may NOT say "over D3". The resulting theorem is family-scoped, not
    universal — and this must appear in the verdict language, not only in a
    footnote. Authoring the scope boundary is real work (small).
  BUYS: uses a mechanism ALREADY SEALED INSIDE F'-5, so it establishes no
    new principle and needs no amendment; R-L0 becomes satisfiable within
    the scope; and it forces the universal-vs-represented distinction into
    the verdict itself, which is instance 1 of this program's standing
    tripwire.

OPTION 4 — DEFER UNTIL R-L2b RESOLVES.
  COST: this is the option the clause forbids in substance. Deferring means
    the scoping question is answered by whatever execution produces. It also
    keeps E1 frozen, since E-Q1 blocks by being unruled.
  BUYS: nothing that Option 1 or Option 3 does not buy sooner.
```
