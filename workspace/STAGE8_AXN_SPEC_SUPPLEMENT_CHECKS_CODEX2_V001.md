## 2. Task A1 — the fermionic trace-class criterion [CLAIMED]

### 2.1 Re-derivation [CLAIMED]

For a finite-rank self-adjoint one-particle operator
`T=sum_i lambda_i |f_i><f_i|`, the CAR give

```text
n_i := a*(f_i)a(f_i),       n_i^2=n_i,       [n_i,n_j]=0,
dGamma(T)=sum_i lambda_i n_i.
```

The joint spectrum of the commuting projections is `{0,1}^r`. Therefore

```text
spec dGamma(T) = { sum_(i in S) lambda_i : S subset {1,...,r} },
min spec = -sum_i lambda_i^- ,
max spec =  sum_i lambda_i^+ ,
||dGamma(T)|| = max(sum_i lambda_i^+, sum_i lambda_i^-).
```

Here `lambda^-:=max(-lambda,0)`. For trace-class self-adjoint `T`, finite spectral truncations
converge in trace norm, and

```text
||dGamma(T)-dGamma(T_N)|| <= ||T-T_N||_1.
```

`||dGamma(T)||<=||T||_1`. Conversely, boundedness of `dGamma(T)` bounds every finite positive and
negative spectral sum. Taking the supremum over finite-rank spectral subspaces makes both
