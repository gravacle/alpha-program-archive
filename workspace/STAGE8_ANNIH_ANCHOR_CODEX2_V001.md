## 2. Step 1 — exact projector, independently re-derived

ECO supplies the distributional kernel

```text
C(r) = (1/2) delta^3(r) I - i alpha·r/(2 pi^2 |r|^4).
```

With `f_hat(k)=int exp(-ik·r)f(r)d^3r`, the exact three-dimensional identities give

```text
FT(|r|^-2) = 2 pi^2/|k|,
r/|r|^4 = -(1/2) grad(|r|^-2),
FT(r/|r|^4) = -i pi^2 k_hat,
C_hat(k) = (1/2)(I-alpha·k_hat).
```

E1 independently pins this same momentum-space object as
`C(p)=(I-alpha·p_hat)/2` and types it as `1_(-infinity,0)(h_0)`. For Dirac alpha matrices,

```text

```

No scale-bearing inference is needed. The overall Fourier-sign convention merely swaps the two
complementary band projectors and leaves every following zero intact.

## 3. Step 2 — exact transversality of the stated surrogate



