## 0. Lead Verdict

**SPLIT: a complete role-realizing replacement presentation is admissible;
an anonymous or partial substitute is not.**

The package does not require a uniquely reconstructed microscopic ontology or
one privileged internal representation of `B0`. Its own Q-52 role specification
defines `B0` as an abstract single-origin candidate whose type may be an
operator, flow, family, relation, algebraic generator, or another fully
declared object. What the package actually consumes is the output of

```text
DESCEND_B0 : B0_candidate
  -> SingleOriginPackageInputs(C0,U1,U2,U3,d_C0,d_U1,d_U2,d_U3).
```

A target-independent construction trace that supplies that complete codomain,
all four descent witnesses, the required certificates, and the no-hidden-
supplementation property can therefore realize the `B0` role without uniquely
determining every microscopic feature. This is the sense in which a complete
replacement suffices.

tests require the carrier, state, action, sources, quotient, measure, and
domains to share one declared provenance and to descend before response output
is inspected. A presentation containing only downstream objects, even if they
are mutually compatible, fails the direction/provenance requirement. Under the
package vocabulary, a successful complete replacement is an admissible
realizer of `B0`, not a permission to omit the role.

## 4. The Four Physical Package Requirements

The question "origin or presentation?" has the same answer for all four, but
at two different levels:

1. **Extensional mathematical content:** a complete certified presentation is
   sufficient; unique microscopic reconstruction is not used.
2. **Provenance discipline:** every physical datum must be tied to one frozen
   construction trace through complete descent witnesses; mere co-location is
   insufficient.

### 4.1 Requirement (a): bilocal G with physical inverse

The package does not obtain this from C0 alone. The complete chain is:

```text
C0 carrier/domain/source maps
+ U2 action/evolution/state/effects/contact rules
+ U3 physical quotient, prescription, and operator domains
-> item 1 normalized inclusive functional
-> D1 Log_0 germ
-> D2 raw connected bilocal G
-> D3/D4 contacts and Ward/endpoint data
-> D5 physical two-sided inverse domain.
```

This requires a certified presentation of the listed data and proofs. It does
not require an injective reconstruction of the unique microscopic source. Its
origin-sensitive content is the common-provenance/no-supplementation
certificate.

### 4.2 Requirement (b): Log_0 and contour measure

U3 supplies the predeclared contour prescription and descended measure. It
does **not** itself supply `Log_0`. The nonzero differentiable `Log_0` germ is
D1, proved after item 1. A complete certified presentation of U3 plus the item-1
and D1 proof suffices extensionally. Again, the measure/prescription cannot be
chosen after inspecting the output.

### 4.3 Requirement (c): branch metric, reality, and DeWitt conventions

The formal convention can exist before `B0`; the physical implementation is
U1 on C0. This is the least origin-sensitive requirement in content, but its
physical representation and compatibility must still be predeclared and
natural under the package's allowed representation changes. A certified U1
presentation suffices; unique microscopic origin does not.

### 4.4 Requirement (d): contacts, boundary data, Ward endpoints

U2/U3 supply the input contact rules, boundary/edge/gluing data, and endpoint
operator domains. The differentiated contacts are D3 and the Ward identities
and endpoint intertwiners are D4 after item 1/D1/D2 exist. A certified
presentation plus these downstream derivations suffices extensionally. The
input rules and domains must share the frozen package provenance.

### 4.5 Core split

None of (a)-(d) requires **unique full microscopic origin determination**.
All four require **complete common-origin provenance and direction-bearing
descent**, because the package's anti-fitting tests inspect when and from where
their inputs enter.

The relevant split is therefore not `(a),(b)` versus `(c),(d)`. It is:

```text
physical structure/properties    -> presentation sufficient
single-origin/no-supplementation -> complete provenance and descent required
```
