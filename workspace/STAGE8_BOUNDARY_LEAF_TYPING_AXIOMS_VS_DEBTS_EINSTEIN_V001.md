computed alpha's grade is capped by the floor's state — today it would be *conditional on eight
adoptions the corpus's own sealed text says were owed as derivations*, which is exactly the

grade. They differ only by the process certificates (freeze, sweep, root-counterfactual) that a
derivation needs on top of a clean floor — **so the register should treat them as one workstream
with two exit criteria, not two questions.**
