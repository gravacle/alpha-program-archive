## 2. THE FORCED PART — a stabilizer triple, derived from membership alone

A direction at an admitted `L_c` is a perturbation `δL_c` such that the perturbed density **remains
an admitted primitive interaction density for the same incidence `c`**. Everything forced follows
from that and from CIS's own typing. `Omega_c` is parent-assigned data per incidence and is **not**
varied — perturbing `L_c` does not perturb its cell.

**D1 — SUPPORT PRESERVATION. FORCED.**
Admission requires `support(L_c) ⊆ Omega_c`. If `L_c + εδL_c` is to be admitted for the same `c`,
its support must also lie in `Omega_c` for the perturbation to be a direction *within the class*.
Since `Omega_c` is fixed,

```text
support(delta L_c)  subset  Omega_c.
```

CIS's falsifier *"the assigned support is not Lorentz-covariant in the declared ordinary branch"*
is satisfied automatically: `Omega_c` is the parent's Lorentz-covariant cell and is unchanged, so a
perturbation confined to it inherits the covariance rather than needing its own.

**D2 — ONE-USE PRESERVATION. FORCED, AND IMPLIED BY D1 — a derived redundancy.**
CIS's one-use rule removes a completed incidence from the active generator past its closure face, and
forbids reapplying it to its old record factor. A perturbation satisfying D1 is confined to `Omega_c`
and therefore **cannot act past the closure face**, and it modifies the single use rather than adding
a second. **So one-use imposes no direction constraint beyond D1.**

This is worth stating rather than listing: **two of CIS's three typing clauses collapse into one at
the level of directions.** A reader tallying "three CIS clauses, three direction constraints" would
overcount the relation's content.

**D3 — RECORD-FACTOR TARGETING. FORCED, AND GENUINELY INDEPENDENT.**
CIS permits shared *source* support but forbids a cell acting on an **earlier record factor**. This
constrains not where `δL_c` lives but **what it writes on**:

```text
delta L_c  may write only on c's OWN new record factor;
it may not induce action on any earlier record factor.
```

D3 is not implied by D1: a perturbation supported inside `Omega_c` could still, in principle, couple
to a record factor completed earlier if `Omega_c` overlaps that factor's cell. **D1 is a support
condition; D3 is a target condition.** They are independent, and D3 is the one clause of CIS that
survives the collapse in D2.

### 2.1 What the forced part IS, structurally — CLAIMED

```text
FORCED DIRECTION RELATION  =  { delta L_c : support(delta L_c) subset Omega_c,
                                            and delta L_c writes only on c's own new record factor }
```

**And its character is the finding.** Every forced clause is a **PRESERVATION** condition — it says
what a perturbation must not break. **CIS is a conservation-of-typing law, so it yields a STABILIZER
condition, not a GENERATION condition.**

A direction relation adequate to define a variation needs two things:

```text
(i)  WHICH DIRECTIONS EXIST   — supplied by the carrier A_c's form
(ii) WHICH ARE LAWFUL         — supplied by CIS
```

**CIS supplies (ii) completely and is silent on (i).** That is exactly why SC4 stays open, and it is
consistent with 847's typing rather than an alternative to it: `A_c`'s form is precisely "which
directions exist", and 847 records that neither the finite-generator branch nor the continuum-carrier
branch is forced.
