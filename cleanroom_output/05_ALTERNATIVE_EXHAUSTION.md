# 05 — Alternative Exhaustion (Gate C)

Seven physically admissible alternative families survive at load-bearing
choices. For each: the family, its free datum, and whether any supplied
principle provides a target-independent selector. Quotes are from package
files.

## Family A — Closure-action magnitude family  ⛔ no selector

Local, proper-Lorentz-scalar, U(1)_rel-neutral, real, bounded-below,
coefficient-free closure potentials for r = (a_R + i b_R)/E_*.
Exhibited members: V1 = (|r|²−1)², V2 = (|r|²−2)² → m_free c² = E_* vs √2E_*.
Free datum: the discrete functional form itself.
Selector: **NONE** — "The existing Gravacle principles do not derive which
formula, if either, is physical. Calling one formula 'minimal' would not
select it." The paired-return principle selects the FIELD, not the action.
This is the live mass-shifting freedom blocking SP08 item 7.

## Family B — Closure-background phase orbit  ⛔ no completed selector

Continuous phase of (a_R, b_R) at fixed |r_*|; free datum: one angle.
Candidate resolver (CP rule) is branch-scoped to the source bilinear in the
declared zero-index regulator branch; the closure-field dynamics containing
the phase is not constructed; CP is not derived as universal law.

## Family C — Absolute record interval  ⛔ no selector

T_R ∈ (0, ∞). Everything derived is relational: τ_R = π/√2 (dimensionless),
m_*T_R = π, E_*T_R = πħ, marker πħ, budget πħ/2. The fundamental principle's
unique-interval clause is an existence **postulate**
(`unique_causal_record_cell_derived = false`). "The absolute SI value of T_R
is not inserted."

## Family D — CTP state/contour family  ⛔ adopted only

The stationary quasifree positive-energy state (⇒ Feynman +i0) is a
**disclosed adopted branch input**; the full Schwinger–Keldysh matrix is
open; alternative states/contours are not excluded.

## Family E — Connected many-record generator family  ⚠ adopted-premise selector only

Cross-cell overlap kernels: B_λ counterfamily (5 distinct spectra, identical
vacuum/one-record restrictions — executable-confirmed); ordering
(continuous-sum/time-ordered/sequential/Trotter/circuit) and preparation
freedoms; quartic competitor H_K + λnᵢnⱼ rejected **only** by the adopted
quasi-free completeness premise ("It does not follow from CAR, locality, or
disjoint monoidality alone").

## Family F — Parent zero-form / primitive-operator competitors  ⚠ adopted-premise selector only

Normal-dependent root/endpoint zero-forms (slash(n_r), slash(n_r)γ₅);
intrinsic tangential competitor retained with nullity eight; endpoint U(2)
element unresolved (mandatory negative control); independent primitive
Pauli/curvature coefficients excluded **only** by the adopted Single-Operator
Completeness premise ("not a derived theorem").
`complete_parent_zero_form_family_enumerated = FALSE`.

## Family G — Enlarged incidence/branch families  ⛔ no exclusion claimed

Parallel paths, public intermediates, loops, faces ("No universal exclusion
of enlarged branches is claimed"); non-CP-even / nonzero-index branches with
unresolved axial phase; **additional vectorlike pairs** ("consistent and…
not proved impossible").

## Contrast: where a genuine target-independent selector exists

The action-multiplier c = 1 has a genuine faithfulness-based selector
(verified executable; 25 characters) — but it is **conditional** on a
normalized completed-record amplitude that does not yet exist
(`complete_Q_spec_amplitude_derived = false`), and this run found its
non-integer-c exclusion step logically incomplete for the actual competitor
family (|Z|^c is single-valued for every real c > 0; the integer restriction
binds only the complex power under the contested Im-W convention). The
spec's less-favorable flag `primitive_action_multiplier_fixed = false`
controls.

## Stop-rule assessment

Families A and C alone satisfy the stop rule (two or more physically
admissible alternatives; supplied principles contain no target-independent
selector — by the package's own explicit statements). Families B, D, G add
further unselected freedoms; E and F are closed only by adopted premises,
supporting at most BRANCH_CONDITIONAL semantics for results scoped inside
those premises. **Verdict: BLOCKED**, with NEEDS_THEORY_DECISION.md issued.
The package-honest caveat is preserved: A is a nonselection witness, not a
proof of permanent underdetermination.
